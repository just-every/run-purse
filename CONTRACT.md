# Purse product contract

Target edition: 17 October 2026. Purse is connected procurement for growing US businesses (50–500 employees) that replaces Procurify, and gives a buyer of Coupa's mid-market purchase-to-pay or Precoro a complete switch, with the intake orchestration and agent depth buyers look for in Zip and Levelpath. Employees ask for what they need on the web, in Slack or Microsoft Teams, on a phone or from an Amazon Business or other PunchOut catalog; suppliers are onboarded with W-9, insurance and verified bank details, and a supplier a requester asks for that is not yet in the register is proposed on the request and passes a vendor approval chain before anyone can order from it; every line is coded, split across departments, locations, projects and GL accounts where a cost is shared, and carries the business's own custom fields such as grant or event codes; budgets and approval chains (including approver pools where the first member to act decides) hold spend before it is committed; purchase orders are actually sent, acknowledged and chased; goods and services are received; supplier invoices arrive by email, are read, de-duplicated and matched two- or three-way, with deposits billed before delivery and applied once against the final bill; approvers, buyers and AP talk on the record in internal comments with @mentions; controllers close each month with a received-not-billed accrual; card-paid purchases are funded at the card provider and reconciled back to their request, PO and budget; and vendors, POs, bills and payment status stay in sync with QuickBooks Online, QuickBooks Desktop, Xero, NetSuite, Sage Intacct, Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central or [Pantry](https://github.com/just-every/run-pantry) without double posting, including on the first sync after a move from Procurify. Money is USD by default (other currencies stay separate), dates and reminders follow US time zones and daylight saving, and fiscal years, 1099 reporting, sales and use tax and ACH follow US norms.

This is a target contract, not a release verdict: it describes the product Purse is being built towards so the distance can be measured. Every named clause states the behavior a buyer gets and the acceptance evidence (a journey or test through a public door) that proves it. The status table at the end marks each clause `done`, `partial` or `missing` against current code and tests; a cited local test proves the bounded local behavior only, never hosted placement, provider receipt or a real owner export. Clause IDs PUR-001 to PUR-017 are kept unchanged. This documentation round changes no homepage, interface or runtime behavior.

Purse is one product among sibling seeds and keeps the market's separation: [Pantry](https://github.com/just-every/run-pantry) is the general ledger, [Coaster](https://github.com/just-every/run-coaster) owns employee expenses, corporate cards and reimbursements, [Locket](https://github.com/just-every/run-locket) owns contract lifecycle management, [Inkwell](https://github.com/just-every/run-inkwell) owns e-signature, [Pier](https://github.com/just-every/run-pier) owns warehouse inventory, [Easel](https://github.com/just-every/run-easel) owns financial planning, [Auth](https://github.com/just-every/run-auth) is a workforce identity provider and [Linen](https://github.com/just-every/run-linen) is the IT service desk. Purse integrates with each of them, and with their incumbents, rather than rebuilding them.

## Purchasing core (existing guarantees)

- **PUR-001 — Business setup and scoped capabilities.** One business retains locations, cost centers, projects, currencies and native-session requester, approver, buyer and receiver capabilities. Setup preserves identity and data. The full role model is ACCESS-001's. **Acceptance evidence:** Sign in as genuine members, configure the business and capabilities, re-run setup and restart; each member keeps exactly the configured capabilities and no data is lost (`tests/purse.test.mjs` P01).

- **PUR-002 — Supplier register with blocking.** Suppliers retain contacts, documents, agreements and preferred/blocked reasons. Blocked suppliers cannot enter native purchasing. Self-service onboarding, tax and bank details are SUPPLIER-001 to SUPPLIER-003's. **Acceptance evidence:** Create, prefer and block suppliers through the API; submission, approval, conversion and issue to a blocked supplier fail with the recorded reason (P02).

- **PUR-003 — Approved catalog prices.** Approved catalog reference prices copy into request revisions; subsequent catalog changes do not change approved terms. **Acceptance evidence:** Request a catalog item, change its price after approval and see the approved revision and PO keep the original price (P03).

- **PUR-004 — Durable exact request revisions.** Durable drafts retain decimal quantities, project/category/need date, real attachment bytes and immutable revision history. Invalid values are refused. **Acceptance evidence:** Create and amend a request with attachments, download the original bytes and read every revision; invalid quantities and dates are refused without writes (P04).

- **PUR-005 — Quotation comparison.** Supplied quotations compare price, freight, tax, delivery, conditions and evidence, with missing fields visible and currencies separate. Supplier-submitted responses are SOURCE-002's. **Acceptance evidence:** Record quotes in two currencies with a missing freight value; the comparison never merges currencies and shows the missing field (P05).

- **PUR-006 — Exact-revision policy and concurrent budgets.** Current amount/category/project policy binds the exact request revision. Self approval fails. Concurrent budget reservations cannot overspend. Multi-step chains are APPROVAL-001's. **Acceptance evidence:** Approve the exact revision under policy, attempt self approval and race two reservations against one budget; only one fits (P06).

- **PUR-007 — Decisions and scoped delegation.** Approve/reject/return decisions retain reason, actor, time, revision and delegation. Scoped delegation expires. Resubmission preserves history. **Acceptance evidence:** Return, resubmit and approve under an expiring delegation; after expiry the delegate is refused and the history keeps every decision (P07).

- **PUR-008 — Approved lines become numbered POs once.** Approved lines allocate once to numbered POs with source links and downloadable documents, including concurrent conversion. **Acceptance evidence:** Convert the same approved lines concurrently; one PO receives them and its document downloads (P08).

- **PUR-009 — Issuance and amendments under renewed authority.** Document generation and authorized issuance are distinct. Revisions and POs retain approved supplier identity and terms across supplier-directory edits. Supplier/quantity/cost amendments retain issued history, invalidate stale authority, reserve changes and require renewed approval before an amendment is issued. Sending to the supplier is PO-001's. **Acceptance evidence:** Issue a PO, edit the supplier directory, amend supplier and cost; the old issuance remains, the amendment needs fresh approval and reserves its change (P09).

- **PUR-010 — Receiving, returns and commitments.** Partial receipts, service milestones, rejection, return and cancellation retain reasons. Events explain outstanding quantities and commitments. Physical returns exceeding current replenishment authority remain recorded with an explicit hold for review, without overspending. Overreceipt, repeat return and wrong roles fail. **Acceptance evidence:** Receive partially, reject, return beyond authority and cancel; outstanding quantities and commitments reconcile and invalid receipts are refused (P10).

- **PUR-011 — Invoice evidence, discrepancies and accounting CSV.** Supplier ID plus invoice reference identifies invoice evidence once across POs. Identical re-entry is idempotent; conflicting evidence is refused for review. Allocations expose price/quantity/receipt discrepancies and export accounting CSV once without posting or payment. Live posting is SYNC-001 to SYNC-004's and payment is AP-001's. **Acceptance evidence:** Enter an invoice twice identically and once conflicting, allocate across POs and download the CSV; discrepancies show and the conflict is refused (P11).

- **PUR-012 — Commitments, overdue and renewals visible; complete restore.** Commitments remain currency-separated, overdue receiving and renewals stay visible, and complete `.rer` archive restore preserves domain links, decisions, files and import history. Corrupt/nonempty restore fails atomically. **Acceptance evidence:** Export a populated installation, restore into an empty one and compare records and files; corrupt or nonempty restores write nothing (`tests/browser-archive.mjs`, overview in `src/ui/overview.tsx`).

- **PUR-013 — Reviewed CSV move-in.** The documented first CSV move-in kit uses Base import-kit D1, current native-session SQL guards, exact source keys/bytes/mapping decisions (except SUPPLIER-003's redacted TIN/bank cells with original digest and column custody), no-write preview, reviewed commit, resume, idempotence and conflict-preserving undo. Imported status never grants authority. Real incumbent exports are IMPORT-001 and IMPORT-002's. **Acceptance evidence:** Preview, change input, commit, resume and undo a fixture CSV; changed input needs a new review and undo preserves edited targets (P13).

- **PUR-014 — Governed human preparation and proposed actions.** Five human preparation workflows (intake, RFQ, renewal, exception, negotiation) retain editable downloadable drafts, missing fields, source references and input versions. ProposedActions recheck current authority at execution. No result is presented as an agent's unless an agent produced it, and nothing is sent or bound without the authority that action requires. The agents that do this work are AGENT-001 to AGENT-011's, governed by AGENT-010. **Acceptance evidence:** Prepare, edit and download each workflow's draft, then execute a proposed action with stale input and with the wrong role; both are refused (P14).

- **PUR-015 — Owner-run source and command path.** MIT source runs through maintained RER install/setup/dev/build/preview/verify/reset/export/import/deploy-dry-run/describe/check, with native TanStack Start/Hono/Cloudflare, shipped fonts, Purse theme and real dev personas. **Acceptance evidence:** Run the RER command path from a fresh clone and the browser journey (`tests/runtime.mjs`, `tests/browser.mjs`, `tests/run-site.mjs`).

- **PUR-016 — Exact money and quantities with an append-only ledger.** Quantities are thousandths; money is integer minor units, each line rounds half-up once. Commitment reservations/releases/amendments append to an auditable ledger. Invalid/nonfinite values fail. **Acceptance evidence:** Enter decimal quantities and currency text, round lines and amend; the commitment ledger is append-only and invalid values leave state unchanged (P16 and the currency-text test).

- **PUR-017 — Current-session authorization on every door.** Every private API/download checks current native session, capability, business and record scope. Forged actor, role, revision and total cannot authorize. Denials preserve domain state. **Acceptance evidence:** Call every command and download signed out, with the wrong role, forged revision, revoked session and another record's scope; each is refused without mutation (P17).

### Intake

- **INTAKE-001 — Guided intake by category.** Admins build intake forms per purchase type (goods, software and subscriptions, professional services, contractors, equipment) with conditional questions, required attachments (specifications, quotes, security questionnaires), default coding (with a default CODING-001 split template where a cost is shared), the CODING-002 custom header and line fields that purchase type requires, and a requested need date. The employee path is a single short form that hides fields their purchase does not need, shows which policy and approvers will apply before submission and keeps a draft if they leave. A requester can copy any past request or PO they can see into a new draft ("request again"): lines, supplier, coding, CODING-001 splits and CODING-002 field values copy, catalog lines take the current catalog price with any change from the original shown (PUR-003), and attachments copy only if the requester ticks them; approvals, quotes, receipts and invoices never copy, the draft is a new request routed afresh under APPROVAL-001, and a catalog item outside the requester's current access (CATALOG-001), a blocked supplier (PUR-002) or an inactive coding or field value is flagged and left out until replaced. When the supplier a requester needs is not in the register, the form lets them propose one on the request (SUPPLIER-008) rather than leaving Purse, and the draft and submission carry the pending supplier. **Acceptance evidence:** Configure a software form with a conditional security question, fill it as a requester on a phone, see the predicted approval route before submission and submit; the request revision carries every answer and file. Half-fill another request with an attachment, close the tab and sign in from a second device: the draft is there with its answers and file, and nothing was submitted. Copy last month's approved request for three catalog items, one of whose prices has since risen and one of which is no longer in the requester's catalog access: the new draft holds the other two lines with their coding and split, shows the price change, flags the third item and carries no approval, and submitting it routes afresh. On a goods form, type a caterer that is not in the register: the form offers SUPPLIER-008's proposal fields, the draft saves with the pending supplier, and the predicted route shows the new-supplier step. Before shipping new roles, keys or intake forms, preserve INTAKE-004 current requester/admin authority, private email-source reads, reasoned held-mail assignment, explicit draft completion and durable replay/mail outcomes; acceptance must assert these obligations through the new public door.

- **INTAKE-002 — Slack intake, approvals and updates.** A Purse Slack app lets employees start a request with a shortcut or message, attach files, and follow its status in a thread; approvers approve, reject or return the exact revision from an interactive message after sign-in binding; requesters receive PO, acknowledgment, delivery and chase updates. Slack actions use the same server-side authority as the web. **Acceptance evidence:** Install the app in a test workspace, raise a request from Slack, approve it from the approval message, amend it and see the stale message refuse to approve; status updates arrive in the thread. Before shipping Slack, carry RECEIVE-001 receiver prompts and sign-in-bound partial/full receipt replies with the same current assignment and stale-position refusal; acceptance must assert both.

- **INTAKE-003 — Microsoft Teams intake, approvals and updates.** The same journey as INTAKE-002 through a Teams app (Bot Framework with Adaptive Cards), installable by a Teams admin, with approval cards bound to the exact revision. **Acceptance evidence:** Sideload the Teams app in a test tenant, raise and approve a request from Teams cards, and see a stale card refused and updates delivered to the chat. Before shipping Teams, carry RECEIVE-001 receiver prompts and sign-in-bound partial/full receipt replies with the same current assignment and stale-position refusal; acceptance must assert both.

- **INTAKE-004 — Email intake.** Each business has a request address; an employee forwards a supplier quote or describes a need by email and receives a link to a prefilled draft with the attachments retained. Mail from unknown senders is held, never turned into a request. The owner records the address and configures the signed Resend receiving webhook and private provider credentials; recording an address does not verify or configure a mailbox. The current supported door verifies the exact webhook bytes and freshness, hydrates the signed provider email ID through Base's real receiving adapter, and privately retains the exact provider JSON and decoded attachments in R2. The provider-reported sender label never claims verified employee authorship. The subject prefills the draft title, original body/HTML remain source evidence, and the assigned requester completes supplier, coding, lines and terms before explicitly creating one ordinary request draft; submission and approval remain separate native actions. Known senders require current native requester capability (the owner can request); unknown, inactive and non-requester senders are held for the owner's reasoned assignment to an active requester, with no implied grant. Until ACCESS-001 supplies admin, the owner performs administration. Only the owner and current assigned requester read the intake and its exact download-only originals, including after completion; only its assigned requester edits/completes it. Current-session and revision checks apply at each action. Source content is data, never instructions. Provider email identity durably de-duplicates ingestion, and draft-link mail claims before sending with honest adapter outcomes; uncertain claims are visible to the owner and never silently repeated. Bounds are three attachments of 10 MB (10,000,000 bytes) each, 30 MB decoded total, 500 kB combined body and 2 MB provider JSON, refused without truncation. Complete archives retain source bytes, source/request lineage, receipt ledger, holds, corrections, assignments and mail outcomes; archived principals grant no native session. ACCESS-001, API-001 and INTAKE-001 inherit current-role source privacy, explicit completion and replay rules before adding roles, keys or form capabilities; ERASURE-001 inherits the new personal source fields and originals. **Acceptance evidence:** Send a quote PDF from a member's address to the request address and from an unknown address; the member gets a prefilled draft link and the unknown mail is held. Through native HTTP and the web at 1440 and 390, inspect exact private source downloads, keep unknown mail inaccessible to another requester, assign with a reason, correct the draft, complete required purchasing fields into one request draft and submit separately. Wrong-role, inactive, signed-out and stale or repeated actions are refused; invalid/stale webhook signatures do not fetch or write, a repeated provider message creates no second intake/send, and planted source instructions grant no authority. Complete export/restore preserves exact originals and receipt/lineage/mail evidence. The real Base inbound/outbound adapters run with provider network-edge fakes under ct's 19 October 18:06Z ruling; no mailbox delivery or provider sandbox claim is made. Evidence: `tests/email-intake.test.mjs`; `tests/browser-email-intake.mjs`; `tests/browser.mjs`.

- **INTAKE-005 — Cross-team review orchestration.** A purchase type can require reviews by IT security, legal, privacy or finance in parallel or sequence before approval, with each reviewer's questions, files and decision on the request. Reviews can open and track tickets in Jira, ServiceNow or [Linen](https://github.com/just-every/run-linen), pull a supplier's risk result from OneTrust, and hand contract review to [Locket](https://github.com/just-every/run-locket). **Acceptance evidence:** Submit a new-software request that triggers security, privacy, IT and legal reviews; complete security in Purse, IT through a linked Jira ticket and a linked ServiceNow incident (Jira and ServiceNow developer instances), and a second IT request through a linked [Linen](https://github.com/just-every/run-linen) ticket on a local Linen installation; pull a OneTrust vendor risk result from its sandbox onto the privacy review; hand legal review to a local [Locket](https://github.com/just-every/run-locket) installation and see its decision return. Approval waits until every review is decided, and closing each linked ticket records its decision on the request.

- **INTAKE-006 — Travel pre-approval requests.** Travel is a request type alongside goods, services and subscriptions, as in Procurify: an employee (or an assistant for them) requests pre-approval for a trip with the traveler, purpose, origin and destinations, departure and return dates, and estimated costs by category (airfare, lodging, ground transport, meals and incidentals defaulting to the GSA per diem rate for the destination, registration fees, other), with optional attachments such as a conference agenda. The request routes through APPROVAL-001 chains (for example the manager, then finance above a threshold, then a travel or risk reviewer for international trips) and reserves the estimate against the budget (BUDGET-001) like any request. Approval gives the traveler an approval reference to book with; booking itself happens in the business's travel tool (Navan, TravelPerk, SAP Concur Travel) or on a card funded under CARD-001. Actual trip spend returns from [Coaster](https://github.com/just-every/run-coaster) (or an Expensify, Navan, Ramp or Brex expense export) by that reference and moves the reservation to actual; the unspent reservation releases when the trip's expense report closes, or a configured number of days after the return date. A change of dates or a higher estimate is an amendment that re-routes under PUR-009. **Acceptance evidence:** Submit a three-day trip to Austin with a $2,400 estimate under a chain that adds finance above $2,000; meals default to the GSA per diem for Austin, the manager and finance approve, and the department budget reserves $2,400. Raise the estimate to $2,700 and see it re-route and reserve $2,700 once approved; then return a $2,150 expense report referencing the request from a local Coaster installation and see $2,150 move to actual and $550 released, with the report linked on the request.

- **INTAKE-007 — Integration purchase-request tokens.** The native owner creates a token scoped only to creating purchase-request drafts and binds it to one active native requester. Linen ASSET-005 and Pier STOCK-001 inherit this create-only boundary by name; the wider API-001 remains separate. Tokens contain 32 random bytes, are displayed once, and only SHA-256 digests are stored. The owner can rotate or revoke a token immediately; old/revoked tokens cannot create or replay requests. Bearers, preparation and proposed agent actions cannot create, rotate or revoke tokens, read workspace/source records, submit, approve, issue, receive or pay. Token use rechecks the bound native identity and current requester capability; inactive or no-longer-requester identities are refused. The owner cannot silently change the binding: create a new token for a different requester. `POST /api/purse/integration/requests` requires a bearer in the Authorization header, an Idempotency-Key and the ordinary complete purchasing fields. It creates one native draft under the bound requester, recording the token label and requester on every draft, preserving source integration identity and applying normal supplier, catalog, coding, amount and line validation. Client identity, approval and totals confer no authority. As with INTAKE-004, a human reviews and explicitly submits before normal policy approval; creating a draft makes no decision, reservation, PO or send. The same token integration and Idempotency-Key with identical UTF-8 JSON bytes return the original creation response, including after rotation or later human edits; a changed body returns 409 without another draft, and concurrent retries create once. Keys contain 1–200 visible ASCII characters; the body limit is 1,600,000 bytes with no truncation. Tokens and replay-response authority never enter portable archives; complete restore preserves purchasing lineage and AUDIT-001 create/rotate/revoke/use/refusal evidence but requires fresh owner-issued tokens. Every refused use is audited with outcome/reason, door and the recognized integration label/requester when available; invalid or rotated secrets are anonymous, never retained. Secrets and digests never appear in ordinary state, token lists, audit read/export, agent context or URLs. **Acceptance evidence:** `tests/integration-tokens.test.mjs` names INTAKE-007 and asserts native owner issuance, requester binding, bearer-only scope, forged authority refusal, simultaneous same/changed-body idempotency, ordinary human submission/approval, role/native deactivation, rotation/revocation, audit and complete archive restore without credentials. `tests/browser-integration-tokens.mjs` exercises Settings issuance/rotation/revocation and the requester's draft/submission at 1440 and 390 pixels.

### Suppliers

- **SUPPLIER-001 — Self-service supplier onboarding.** A buyer invites a supplier by email, or the invite starts from a SUPPLIER-008 new-supplier proposal (sent by the buyer, or automatically where the business turns on auto-invite), to a no-account onboarding link where the supplier enters legal name, DBA, entity type, address, contacts, remit-to, W-9 (TIN, federal tax classification, backup-withholding certification and e-signature), certificate of insurance, diversity certificates and payment terms. The submission is reviewed and activated by the buyer or, where the business configures one, through SUPPLIER-008's vendor approval chain, with SUPPLIER-002, SUPPLIER-003 and SUPPLIER-007 checks still in force; the supplier can return through a fresh link to update details, which then go back to review. The supplier-link door must preserve SUPPLIER-004’s private single-use document refresh, buyer review, reminder cycles and required-document PO holds. **Acceptance evidence:** Refresh an expiring document through the supplier link under SUPPLIER-004 and retain its private history and current hold until buyer review. Invite a supplier, complete onboarding from the link on a phone with a W-9 and COI, review and activate; the supplier becomes purchasable and its documents download. With a vendor approval chain configured, the same submission waits for the chain and the buyer alone cannot activate it. ### Approvals and budgets

- **APPROVAL-001 — Multi-step conditional approval chains.** Chains combine sequential and parallel steps chosen by amount, department, location, project, category, GL account, entity, CODING-002 custom field values, supplier risk, new-supplier status (true while the request's supplier is a SUPPLIER-008 pending supplier) and the requester's manager hierarchy (from ACCESS-002 or the member record; a manager change re-routes steps not yet decided to the new manager); rules on a coded dimension are evaluated for each CODING-001 allocation of a split line. Sequential steps open one after another; parallel steps open together, and the chain moves on only when every one of them is decided. Each step is either "all of" (every named approver or reviewer must approve, in any order) or "any one of" a named group or pool, as Procurify's approval pools are: an "any one of" step reaches every member at once on every channel, and the first member to approve, reject or return decides it. When a pool step is decided, Purse withdraws it from the other members' queues, updates their Slack and Teams messages to show who decided and when, and refuses their email links, Slack and Teams actions and native-app actions with that reason (APPROVAL-003); the step records the deciding member. Concurrent decisions on one step are serialized, so exactly one decides it and the others are refused. A pool member who is the requester is skipped and the step still needs another member to decide; if no other active member remains, it escalates as APPROVAL-002 says. Reminders, escalation and out-of-office apply to the pool as a whole (APPROVAL-002). The request shows which rule chose each step and why, and an amendment re-evaluates the chain against the new revision. Every sequential, parallel and pool decision, including the deciding pool member and the policy/route in force, must enter AUDIT-001 before this capability ships. Acceptance must assert this obligation through its public door and AUDIT-001 read/export. **Acceptance evidence:** Configure a chain that adds finance above $10,000 and IT for software; submit requests either side of each condition, see the route and reason before and after submission, and see an amendment re-route. Two members approving one pool step at the same moment through the API: one decides and the other is refused. A request raised by a pool member reaches only the other two members. An "all of" parallel step naming IT and security stays open after IT approves and moves on only when security also approves. - **AGENT-008 — Coding and policy agent.** The agent suggests GL account, class, department and project coding on requests and invoices from history, and flags split purchases that avoid an approval threshold, repeat non-PO spend that should be on a PO, and purchases from a non-preferred supplier when a preferred one exists. People accept or correct suggestions, and corrections improve later suggestions: each accepted or corrected coding is kept as an example for that supplier, item and requester, and later suggestions weigh the most recent corrections above older history and cite them. **Acceptance evidence:** Submit two requests from one requester to one supplier just under a threshold on the same day; the agent flags the split for the approver, and its coding suggestion on a new invoice cites the past invoices it learned from. Correct its GL account suggestion on two invoices from one supplier: on the third invoice from that supplier the agent suggests the corrected account and cites the two corrections.

- **AGENT-009 — Price benchmark and negotiation brief.** For a request or renewal the agent compares quoted unit prices with the business's own purchase history, current PunchOut catalog prices and other quotes received, and drafts a negotiation brief with target price and arguments. It never makes or accepts an offer. **Acceptance evidence:** Request an item bought before at a lower price; the brief cites the earlier PO and the PunchOut price and proposes a target, and no message is sent without the buyer.

- **AGENT-010 — Agent governance.** Every agent action is a PUR-014 ProposedAction checked against current authority at execution, or a pre-authorized message type within its cadence and count limits. Owners enable each agent per purchase type, see each agent's full record (inputs, sources, model and provider used, messages sent, proposals and outcomes), pause any agent at once, and agents never see SECURITY-001's protected fields. Documents and supplier messages are data, never instructions. **Acceptance evidence:** Enable AGENT-003 with a two-chase limit; see it stop at the limit, pause it mid-run and see no further sends, inspect its record, and see an instruction planted in a supplier reply have no effect. Before shipping, SECURITY-001 protected values and suffixes stay outside agent context; protected fields cannot be ProposedAction targets. Assert these boundaries through every new agent door before shipping; this duty alone does not complete overall governance.

- **AGENT-011 — Spend questions in plain language.** A member asks a spend question in the web, Slack or Teams ("what did Marketing commit to software this quarter against budget?", "which suppliers did we pay over $50,000 last year without a PO?"); the agent answers from Purse's own records within that member's ACCESS-001 scope, shows the figures as a table or chart, states the filters and period it used, and links every figure to the requests, POs, invoices and card transactions behind it so it recomputes through REPORT-001. It says when the data cannot answer the question rather than estimating, and never sees SECURITY-001's protected fields. **Acceptance evidence:** With a populated workspace, ask three spend questions as a finance viewer and one as a department-scoped requester; each answer equals the REPORT-001 figure for the stated filters and drills to its records, the requester's answer covers only their scope, and a question the data cannot answer is declined with the reason.

## Honest boundaries

- **Payments and banking.** Purse approves and hands off; it does not hold funds, operate payment rails, issue cards or underwrite. Buyers pay through BILL, their ledger's bill pay or an ACH provider such as Dwolla (AP-001, AP-002) and Purse reconciles the returned status.
- **Employee expenses and cards.** Out-of-pocket expenses, corporate card issuing, card feeds and reimbursements belong to [Coaster](https://github.com/just-every/run-coaster) (or Expensify, Ramp, Brex); Purse shares suppliers, coding and budgets with it, funds cards and limits for approved card-paid purchases at those providers (CARD-001) and reconciles their transactions back to the request, PO and budget (CARD-002). Travel booking belongs to the business's travel tool (Navan, TravelPerk, SAP Concur Travel), which Procurify does not provide either; Purse holds travel pre-approval and its budget reservation (INTAKE-006), and trip expenses return from Coaster or the expense tool.
- **General ledger.** Purse is not the ledger. Accounts, postings, close and financial statements stay in [Pantry](https://github.com/just-every/run-pantry), QuickBooks Online, QuickBooks Desktop, Xero, NetSuite, Sage Intacct or Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central with explicit synchronization (SYNC-001 to SYNC-008).
- **Marketplaces and fulfillment.** Purse does not become Amazon or a distributor; it connects the business's own Amazon Business and supplier PunchOut accounts, and suppliers handle inventory, fulfillment and commercial terms.
- **Contract lifecycle and signatures.** Drafting, redlining and clause playbooks belong to [Locket](https://github.com/just-every/run-locket) or another CLM; signing to [Inkwell](https://github.com/just-every/run-inkwell) or Docusign. Purse holds the agreement record, spend and renewal dates.
- **Warehouse inventory and planning.** Stock across warehouses belongs to [Pier](https://github.com/just-every/run-pier); budgets are planned in [Easel](https://github.com/just-every/run-easel) or a spreadsheet and imported. Purse covers stockroom consumables only (STOCK-001).
- **Tax filing and determination.** Purse does not file 1099s or sales and use tax returns; it prepares the data for the ledger or a filing partner (Track1099, TaxBandits, Avalara). It is not tax or legal advice.
- **Risk guarantees.** Screening, TIN checks and bank verification reduce risk; they do not certify a supplier. Legal and risk owners decide, using OneTrust or their own process where they have one.
- **Autonomous commitments.** Agents prepare, collect, chase and propose; they never negotiate binding terms, accept offers, commit spend or change bank details on their own.
- **US first.** Currencies other than USD stay separate and are supported for foreign suppliers, but VAT, GST, e-invoicing mandates outside the US and non-US tax forms are not in this edition.
- **Owner inputs still required.** An anonymized real purchasing case, an actual Procurify account's Export Data CSVs or API access (IMPORT-001) and a current-process cost and attention baseline come from the owner; acceptance that depends on them stays open until supplied. Existing installations keep applied migrations and use the forward upgrade in `UPGRADING.md`.

## Backup and upgrade

A pre-upgrade export restores only with the Purse release that made it. Restore it
into an empty installation of that release, upgrade that installation’s source
in place, run `rer install` and `rer setup` inside that existing checkout, then export again. Keep the original release
and private export until recovery is verified. Never import an older release’s
archive directly into the current release.
