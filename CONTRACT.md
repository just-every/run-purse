# Purse product contract

Target edition: 17 October 2026. Purse is connected procurement for growing US businesses (50–500 employees) that replaces Procurify, and gives a buyer of Coupa's mid-market purchase-to-pay or Precoro a complete switch, with the intake orchestration and agent depth buyers look for in Zip and Levelpath. Employees ask for what they need on the web, in Slack or Microsoft Teams, on a phone or from an Amazon Business or other PunchOut catalogue; suppliers are onboarded with W-9, insurance and verified bank details; every line is coded, split across departments, locations, projects and GL accounts where a cost is shared, and carries the business's own custom fields such as grant or event codes; budgets and approval chains (including approver pools where the first member to act decides) hold spend before it is committed; purchase orders are actually sent, acknowledged and chased; goods and services are received; supplier invoices arrive by email, are read, de-duplicated and matched two- or three-way, with deposits billed before delivery and applied once against the final bill; approvers, buyers and AP talk on the record in internal comments with @mentions; controllers close each month with a received-not-billed accrual; card-paid purchases are funded at the card provider and reconciled back to their request, PO and budget; and vendors, POs, bills and payment status stay in sync with QuickBooks Online, QuickBooks Desktop, Xero, NetSuite, Sage Intacct, Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central or [Pantry](https://github.com/just-every/run-pantry) without double posting, including on the first sync after a move from Procurify. Money is USD by default (other currencies stay separate), dates and reminders follow US time zones and daylight saving, and fiscal years, 1099 reporting, sales and use tax and ACH follow US norms.

This is a target contract, not a release verdict: it describes the product Purse is being built towards so the distance can be measured. Every named clause states the behaviour a buyer gets and the acceptance evidence (a journey or test through a public door) that proves it. The status table at the end marks each clause `done`, `partial` or `missing` against current code and tests; a cited local test proves the bounded local behaviour only, never hosted placement, provider receipt or a real owner export. Clause IDs PUR-001 to PUR-017 are kept unchanged. This documentation round changes no homepage, interface or runtime behaviour.

Purse is one product among sibling seeds and keeps the market's separation: [Pantry](https://github.com/just-every/run-pantry) is the general ledger, [Coaster](https://github.com/just-every/run-coaster) owns employee expenses, corporate cards and reimbursements, [Locket](https://github.com/just-every/run-locket) owns contract lifecycle management, [Inkwell](https://github.com/just-every/run-inkwell) owns e-signature, [Pier](https://github.com/just-every/run-pier) owns warehouse inventory, [Easel](https://github.com/just-every/run-easel) owns financial planning, [Auth](https://github.com/just-every/run-auth) is a workforce identity provider and [Linen](https://github.com/just-every/run-linen) is the IT service desk. Purse integrates with each of them, and with their incumbents, rather than rebuilding them.

## Product target — 17 October 2026

Purse is the procurement and purchase-to-pay product a growing US business buys instead of Procurify, Coupa's mid-market purchase-to-pay or Precoro: intake, approvals and budgets, POs that are sent and chased, receiving, invoice capture and matching, payment handoff and ledger sync, with agents that do the follow-through. This contract describes that product, which is what Purse is being built towards, and marks how close the current code is (see [Status](#status)).

## Purchasing core (existing guarantees)

- **PUR-001 — Business setup and scoped capabilities.** One business retains locations, cost centres, projects, currencies and native-session requester, approver, buyer and receiver capabilities. Setup preserves identity and data. The full role model is ACCESS-001's. **Acceptance evidence:** Sign in as genuine members, configure the business and capabilities, re-run setup and restart; each member keeps exactly the configured capabilities and no data is lost (`tests/purse.test.mjs` P01).

- **PUR-002 — Supplier register with blocking.** Suppliers retain contacts, documents, agreements and preferred/blocked reasons. Blocked suppliers cannot enter native purchasing. Self-service onboarding, tax and bank details are SUPPLIER-001 to SUPPLIER-003's. **Acceptance evidence:** Create, prefer and block suppliers through the API; submission, approval, conversion and issue to a blocked supplier fail with the recorded reason (P02).

- **PUR-003 — Approved catalogue prices.** Approved catalogue reference prices copy into request revisions; subsequent catalogue changes do not change approved terms. **Acceptance evidence:** Request a catalogue item, change its price after approval and see the approved revision and PO keep the original price (P03).

- **PUR-004 — Durable exact request revisions.** Durable drafts retain decimal quantities, project/category/need date, real attachment bytes and immutable revision history. Invalid values are refused. **Acceptance evidence:** Create and amend a request with attachments, download the original bytes and read every revision; invalid quantities and dates are refused without writes (P04).

- **PUR-005 — Quotation comparison.** Supplied quotations compare price, freight, tax, delivery, conditions and evidence, with missing fields visible and currencies separate. Supplier-submitted responses are SOURCE-002's. **Acceptance evidence:** Record quotes in two currencies with a missing freight value; the comparison never merges currencies and shows the missing field (P05).

- **PUR-006 — Exact-revision policy and concurrent budgets.** Current amount/category/project policy binds the exact request revision. Self approval fails. Concurrent budget reservations cannot overspend. Multi-step chains are APPROVAL-001's. **Acceptance evidence:** Approve the exact revision under policy, attempt self approval and race two reservations against one budget; only one fits (P06).

- **PUR-007 — Decisions and scoped delegation.** Approve/reject/return decisions retain reason, actor, time, revision and delegation. Scoped delegation expires. Resubmission preserves history. **Acceptance evidence:** Return, resubmit and approve under an expiring delegation; after expiry the delegate is refused and the history keeps every decision (P07).

- **PUR-008 — Approved lines become numbered POs once.** Approved lines allocate once to numbered POs with source links and downloadable documents, including concurrent conversion. **Acceptance evidence:** Convert the same approved lines concurrently; one PO receives them and its document downloads (P08).

- **PUR-009 — Issuance and amendments under renewed authority.** Document generation and authorised issuance are distinct. Revisions and POs retain approved supplier identity and terms across supplier-directory edits. Supplier/quantity/cost amendments retain issued history, invalidate stale authority, reserve changes and require renewed approval before an amendment is issued. Sending to the supplier is PO-001's. **Acceptance evidence:** Issue a PO, edit the supplier directory, amend supplier and cost; the old issuance remains, the amendment needs fresh approval and reserves its change (P09).

- **PUR-010 — Receiving, returns and commitments.** Partial receipts, service milestones, rejection, return and cancellation retain reasons. Events explain outstanding quantities and commitments. Physical returns exceeding current replenishment authority remain recorded with an explicit hold for review, without overspending. Overreceipt, repeat return and wrong roles fail. **Acceptance evidence:** Receive partially, reject, return beyond authority and cancel; outstanding quantities and commitments reconcile and invalid receipts are refused (P10).

- **PUR-011 — Invoice evidence, discrepancies and accounting CSV.** Supplier ID plus invoice reference identifies invoice evidence once across POs. Identical re-entry is idempotent; conflicting evidence is refused for review. Allocations expose price/quantity/receipt discrepancies and export accounting CSV once without posting or payment. Live posting is SYNC-001 to SYNC-004's and payment is AP-001's. **Acceptance evidence:** Enter an invoice twice identically and once conflicting, allocate across POs and download the CSV; discrepancies show and the conflict is refused (P11).

- **PUR-012 — Commitments, overdue and renewals visible; complete restore.** Commitments remain currency-separated, overdue receiving and renewals stay visible, and complete `.rer` archive restore preserves domain links, decisions, files and import history. Corrupt/nonempty restore fails atomically. **Acceptance evidence:** Export a populated installation, restore into an empty one and compare records and files; corrupt or nonempty restores write nothing (`tests/browser-archive.mjs`, overview in `src/ui/overview.tsx`).

- **PUR-013 — Reviewed CSV move-in.** The documented first CSV move-in kit uses Base import-kit D1, current native-session SQL guards, exact source keys/bytes/mapping decisions, no-write preview, reviewed commit, resume, idempotence and conflict-preserving undo. Imported status never grants authority. Real incumbent exports are IMPORT-001 and IMPORT-002's. **Acceptance evidence:** Preview, change input, commit, resume and undo a fixture CSV; changed input needs a new review and undo preserves edited targets (P13).

- **PUR-014 — Governed human preparation and proposed actions.** Five human preparation workflows (intake, RFQ, renewal, exception, negotiation) retain editable downloadable drafts, missing fields, source references and input versions. ProposedActions recheck current authority at execution. No result is presented as an agent's unless an agent produced it, and nothing is sent or bound without the authority that action requires. The agents that do this work are AGENT-001 to AGENT-011's, governed by AGENT-010. **Acceptance evidence:** Prepare, edit and download each workflow's draft, then execute a proposed action with stale input and with the wrong role; both are refused (P14).

- **PUR-015 — Owner-run source and command path.** MIT source runs through maintained RER install/setup/dev/build/preview/verify/reset/export/import/deploy-dry-run/describe/check, with native TanStack Start/Hono/Cloudflare, shipped fonts, Purse theme and real dev personas. **Acceptance evidence:** Run the RER command path from a fresh clone and the browser journey (`tests/runtime.mjs`, `tests/browser.mjs`, `tests/run-site.mjs`).

- **PUR-016 — Exact money and quantities with an append-only ledger.** Quantities are thousandths; money is integer minor units, each line rounds half-up once. Commitment reservations/releases/amendments append to an auditable ledger. Invalid/nonfinite values fail. **Acceptance evidence:** Enter decimal quantities and currency text, round lines and amend; the commitment ledger is append-only and invalid values leave state unchanged (P16 and the currency-text test).

- **PUR-017 — Current-session authorisation on every door.** Every private API/download checks current native session, capability, business and record scope. Forged actor, role, revision and total cannot authorise. Denials preserve domain state. **Acceptance evidence:** Call every command and download signed out, with the wrong role, forged revision, revoked session and another record's scope; each is refused without mutation (P17).

## Roles, access and entities

- **ACCESS-001 — Full procurement role model.** Beyond PUR-001's capabilities, a business assigns owner, admin, requester, approver, buyer, receiver, AP clerk, finance viewer and read-only auditor roles, scoped by department, location, project and entity. Requesters see only their own purchases and their department's where granted; AP sees invoices and supplier tax and bank fields only through SECURITY-001's masking; auditors read everything and change nothing. Admins can define custom roles from the same permission set. Every grant and revocation is audited. **Acceptance evidence:** Sign in as each role at 1440 and 390 pixels and through the API; each sees and does exactly its scope, a department-scoped approver cannot act outside it, an auditor's write is refused and the audit shows each grant. Define a custom "Receiving lead" role with only the receive and view-PO permissions for one location and assign it: its holder records a receipt on that location's PO, while their approval attempt, invoice read and receipt on another location's PO are refused through the web and the API.

- **ACCESS-002 — Single sign-on and provisioning.** Owners connect SAML 2.0 or OIDC sign-in with Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace, JumpCloud or [Auth](https://github.com/just-every/run-auth), and SCIM 2.0 provisioning creates, updates and deactivates members with their department and manager. Deactivation ends sessions and transfers open approvals to the manager or a named delegate, so no request stalls on a departed approver. **Acceptance evidence:** Sign in through a test IdP, provision and deprovision a user by SCIM, and see their sessions end and pending approvals move to the delegate with an audit record.

- **ACCESS-003 — Multiple entities and subsidiaries.** One installation holds several US legal entities (or a US parent with foreign subsidiaries), each with its own EIN, ship-to and bill-to addresses, currency, chart of accounts mapping, numbering and accounting connection. Approvers see one cross-entity queue filtered by entity, and a PO cannot be charged to an entity's budget it does not belong to. **Acceptance evidence:** Configure two entities with different accounting connections, raise and approve requests in each from one queue, and see POs numbered and synced to the right ledger; a cross-entity charge is refused.

## Intake

- **INTAKE-001 — Guided intake by category.** Admins build intake forms per purchase type (goods, software and subscriptions, professional services, contractors, equipment) with conditional questions, required attachments (specifications, quotes, security questionnaires), default coding (with a default CODING-001 split template where a cost is shared), the CODING-002 custom header and line fields that purchase type requires, and a requested need date. The employee path is a single short form that hides fields their purchase does not need, shows which policy and approvers will apply before submission and keeps a draft if they leave. A requester can copy any past request or PO they can see into a new draft ("request again"): lines, supplier, coding, CODING-001 splits and CODING-002 field values copy, catalogue lines take the current catalogue price with any change from the original shown (PUR-003), and attachments copy only if the requester ticks them; approvals, quotes, receipts and invoices never copy, the draft is a new request routed afresh under APPROVAL-001, and a catalogue item outside the requester's current access (CATALOG-001), a blocked supplier (PUR-002) or an inactive coding or field value is flagged and left out until replaced. **Acceptance evidence:** Configure a software form with a conditional security question, fill it as a requester on a phone, see the predicted approval route before submission and submit; the request revision carries every answer and file. Half-fill another request with an attachment, close the tab and sign in from a second device: the draft is there with its answers and file, and nothing was submitted. Copy last month's approved request for three catalogue items, one of whose prices has since risen and one of which is no longer in the requester's catalogue access: the new draft holds the other two lines with their coding and split, shows the price change, flags the third item and carries no approval, and submitting it routes afresh.

- **INTAKE-002 — Slack intake, approvals and updates.** A Purse Slack app lets employees start a request with a shortcut or message, attach files, and follow its status in a thread; approvers approve, reject or return the exact revision from an interactive message after sign-in binding; requesters receive PO, acknowledgement, delivery and chase updates. Slack actions use the same server-side authority as the web. **Acceptance evidence:** Install the app in a test workspace, raise a request from Slack, approve it from the approval message, amend it and see the stale message refuse to approve; status updates arrive in the thread.

- **INTAKE-003 — Microsoft Teams intake, approvals and updates.** The same journey as INTAKE-002 through a Teams app (Bot Framework with Adaptive Cards), installable by a Teams admin, with approval cards bound to the exact revision. **Acceptance evidence:** Sideload the Teams app in a test tenant, raise and approve a request from Teams cards, and see a stale card refused and updates delivered to the chat.

- **INTAKE-004 — Email intake.** Each business has a request address; an employee forwards a supplier quote or describes a need by email and receives a link to a prefilled draft with the attachments retained. Mail from unknown senders is held, never turned into a request. **Acceptance evidence:** Send a quote PDF from a member's address to the request address and from an unknown address; the member gets a prefilled draft link and the unknown mail is held.

- **INTAKE-005 — Cross-team review orchestration.** A purchase type can require reviews by IT security, legal, privacy or finance in parallel or sequence before approval, with each reviewer's questions, files and decision on the request. Reviews can open and track tickets in Jira, ServiceNow or [Linen](https://github.com/just-every/run-linen), pull a supplier's risk result from OneTrust, and hand contract review to [Locket](https://github.com/just-every/run-locket). **Acceptance evidence:** Submit a new-software request that triggers security, privacy, IT and legal reviews; complete security in Purse, IT through a linked Jira ticket and a linked ServiceNow incident (Jira and ServiceNow developer instances), and a second IT request through a linked [Linen](https://github.com/just-every/run-linen) ticket on a local Linen installation; pull a OneTrust vendor risk result from its sandbox onto the privacy review; hand legal review to a local [Locket](https://github.com/just-every/run-locket) installation and see its decision return. Approval waits until every review is decided, and closing each linked ticket records its decision on the request.

- **INTAKE-006 — Travel pre-approval requests.** Travel is a request type alongside goods, services and subscriptions, as in Procurify: an employee (or an assistant for them) requests pre-approval for a trip with the traveller, purpose, origin and destinations, departure and return dates, and estimated costs by category (airfare, lodging, ground transport, meals and incidentals defaulting to the GSA per diem rate for the destination, registration fees, other), with optional attachments such as a conference agenda. The request routes through APPROVAL-001 chains (for example the manager, then finance above a threshold, then a travel or risk reviewer for international trips) and reserves the estimate against the budget (BUDGET-001) like any request. Approval gives the traveller an approval reference to book with; booking itself happens in the business's travel tool (Navan, TravelPerk, SAP Concur Travel) or on a card funded under CARD-001. Actual trip spend returns from [Coaster](https://github.com/just-every/run-coaster) (or an Expensify, Navan, Ramp or Brex expense export) by that reference and moves the reservation to actual; the unspent reservation releases when the trip's expense report closes, or a configured number of days after the return date. A change of dates or a higher estimate is an amendment that re-routes under PUR-009. **Acceptance evidence:** Submit a three-day trip to Austin with a $2,400 estimate under a chain that adds finance above $2,000; meals default to the GSA per diem for Austin, the manager and finance approve, and the department budget reserves $2,400. Raise the estimate to $2,700 and see it re-route and reserve $2,700 once approved; then return a $2,150 expense report referencing the request from a local Coaster installation and see $2,150 move to actual and $550 released, with the report linked on the request.

## Mobile

- **MOBILE-001 — Responsive web requesting, approval and receiving.** Requesting, approval status, approval and receiving work in the browser at phone width with readable records and no horizontal scroll. **Acceptance evidence:** Run the fresh-install browser journey at 390 pixels: request, approve and record a partial receipt (`tests/browser-journey.mjs`, `tests/browser.mjs`).

- **MOBILE-002 — Native iOS and Android apps.** Native apps sign in with the business's identity provider, raise a request (including from a catalogue or by photographing a supplier quote, which SOURCE-002 reads into draft lines), push approval, acknowledgement and delivery notifications through APNs and FCM, approve the exact revision, photograph a packing slip or scan a barcode to receive (an item's UPC or GTIN matched to the PO line's supplier part number or catalogue item), and queue receipts offline until they reconnect with conflict checks. **Acceptance evidence:** On test devices, raise a request by photographing a sample quote and confirm its extracted lines, approve it from a push notification, receive offline with a slip photo, reconnect and see the receipt applied once; a revoked session is refused. Scan a sample item's UPC barcode on an open PO: the matching line is selected for receipt, a barcode matching no line on the PO is refused with that reason, and the scanned receipt records the code.

## Suppliers

- **SUPPLIER-001 — Self-service supplier onboarding.** A buyer invites a supplier by email to a no-account onboarding link where the supplier enters legal name, DBA, entity type, address, contacts, remit-to, W-9 (TIN, federal tax classification, backup-withholding certification and e-signature), certificate of insurance, diversity certificates and payment terms. The buyer reviews and activates; the supplier can return through a fresh link to update details, which then go back to review. **Acceptance evidence:** Invite a supplier, complete onboarding from the link on a phone with a W-9 and COI, review and activate; the supplier becomes purchasable and its documents download.

- **SUPPLIER-002 — Bank-detail change controls.** Supplier bank and remit details are entered only by the supplier through SUPPLIER-001's link or by AP with evidence, then verified by a call-back to a previously known contact and an independent second approver before use. Account ownership is checked through a bank-verification provider (Plaid Auth with Plaid Identity Match, or the payment provider's own account verification): the account holder name returned by the provider is compared with the supplier's verified legal name and DBA, and a mismatch, an unverifiable account or a provider error blocks activation of the new details until AP resolves it with evidence, keeping the provider, result, score and time on the supplier. Any change emails the previous contact, places pending payments to that supplier on hold and is audited; a change requested by email alone is never applied. **Acceptance evidence:** Change a supplier's bank details through the link, see payments hold, the old contact notified and activation refused until a second approver records the call-back; an emailed change request cannot alter the details. Replay a provider-sandbox result whose account holder does not match the supplier's legal name: activation stays blocked even after the call-back and second approval, the result is recorded on the supplier, and a matching result then lets the second approver activate.

- **SUPPLIER-003 — Duplicate supplier detection.** New and imported suppliers are checked against existing ones by TIN, normalised legal name and DBA, email domain, address, phone and bank account; possible duplicates are shown before creation and can be merged, keeping both histories. **Acceptance evidence:** Create a supplier differing only by punctuation and one sharing a TIN with an existing record; both are flagged, and a merge keeps every PO and invoice link.

- **SUPPLIER-004 — Document expiry, compliance refresh and renewal reminders.** Insurance certificates, W-9s, licences, security reviews and agreements carry expiry dates; Purse emails the supplier and owner before expiry on a schedule, requests refreshed documents through SUPPLIER-001's link on configured compliance cycles, and can block new POs to a supplier whose required document has lapsed. **Acceptance evidence:** Advance the clock past a COI's reminder and expiry dates; the supplier and owner are emailed, the refreshed COI clears the warning, and a lapsed supplier's new PO is held.

- **SUPPLIER-005 — Supplier performance and issues.** Each supplier shows on-time delivery against promised dates, quality rejections and returns, invoice accuracy (match exceptions per invoice), acknowledgement speed, spend and open issues, with issue records (late, damaged, wrong item, billing) linked to POs and resolved with reasons. **Acceptance evidence:** Record late and rejected deliveries and an invoice mismatch for a supplier; its scorecard reflects each and every figure drills to the records behind it.

- **SUPPLIER-006 — 1099 readiness.** Suppliers carry 1099 eligibility from their W-9, a default 1099-NEC or 1099-MISC box, and backup-withholding status; payments recorded through AP-001 and AP-002 total by calendar year per box and export to the ledger ([Pantry](https://github.com/just-every/run-pantry), QuickBooks Online, QuickBooks Desktop, Xero, NetSuite, Sage Intacct or Business Central, each vendor's 1099 eligibility and box set in that ledger's own 1099 fields through SYNC-001 to SYNC-008) or a filing partner such as Track1099 or TaxBandits. Purse does not file returns. **Acceptance evidence:** Pay 1099 and non-1099 suppliers across a year end; the year's 1099 report totals only eligible payments by box and the export matches the payment records. Sync the 1099 suppliers to a QuickBooks Online sandbox company, a QuickBooks Desktop sample company, a Xero demo company, a NetSuite sandbox, a Sage Intacct sandbox company, a Business Central sandbox and a local Pantry installation: each vendor carries its 1099 eligibility and box in that ledger, and the non-1099 supplier carries none.

- **SUPPLIER-007 — Restricted-party screening.** New suppliers and changes of name or address are screened against the US Treasury OFAC SDN list (through the public list or a screening provider); possible matches block activation for compliance review, and the screening result and source date are kept. **Acceptance evidence:** Onboard a supplier whose name matches a test SDN entry; activation is blocked pending review and the result records the list version.

## Sourcing and catalogues

- **SOURCE-001 — RFQ invitations and supplier responses.** A buyer sends a request for quotation to nominated suppliers with scope, quantities, response deadline and questions; suppliers respond through a no-account link with line prices, freight, tax, lead time, validity and files; reminders go before the deadline; responses land in PUR-005's comparison and the buyer awards one, with the award reason kept. **Acceptance evidence:** Invite three suppliers, have two respond through their links and one miss the deadline after a reminder, compare and award; the request revision carries the awarded quote.

- **SOURCE-002 — Quote document processing.** A supplier quote PDF or email attached to a request is read into draft lines (item, quantity, unit price, freight, tax, lead time, validity) with field confidence; the requester or buyer confirms before the lines enter the revision. **Acceptance evidence:** Attach a sample quote PDF; the extracted lines show confidence, a corrected field is kept, and nothing enters the revision until confirmed.

- **CATALOG-001 — Catalogue items, bundles and access.** Admins and buyers maintain catalogue items (supplier, part number, unit, price with validity dates, default coding, required attachments) and bundles (a named set of items and quantities, such as a new-hire laptop kit, requested as one choice and expanded into request lines). Each item and bundle can be limited to departments, locations, entities or roles; requesters see and can request only what they are granted, and the server refuses a request line for an item outside the requester's access. **Acceptance evidence:** Create an item limited to the Denver location and a bundle of three items for Engineering; a Denver requester sees the item and a Chicago requester does not, an Engineering requester adds the bundle and gets three priced lines, and a direct API request for the restricted item from Chicago is refused.

- **PUNCHOUT-001 — Amazon Business PunchOut.** Requesters punch out from Purse into the business's Amazon Business account (cXML PunchOut), build a cart under its buying policies and return it as request lines with Amazon item IDs and prices; after approval Purse transmits the PO by cXML OrderRequest, and Amazon order confirmations, shipments and invoices flow back to the PO, receipts and invoice matching. **Acceptance evidence:** Against Amazon Business's PunchOut test environment, punch out, return a cart, approve, transmit the PO and receive the confirmation and invoice onto the same PO.

- **PUNCHOUT-002 — Other PunchOut and hosted catalogues.** The same cXML and OCI PunchOut path connects Staples Business Advantage, Grainger, CDW, Office Depot, Uline and Fisher Scientific, and suppliers' hosted catalogues can be loaded from CSV with price validity dates. **Acceptance evidence:** Connect a cXML test catalogue and an OCI one, return carts from each and transmit approved POs; a catalogue CSV with expired prices is refused. Each named supplier (Staples Business Advantage, Grainger, CDW, Office Depot, Uline and Fisher Scientific) then passes the same journey against its own PunchOut test catalogue or test account: punch out, return a cart, transmit the approved PO and receive its order confirmation onto the PO. The clause is done only when all six pass.

- **STOCK-001 — Stockroom consumables.** Office, cleaning and site consumables carry stockroom counts and reorder points; a count below its reorder point creates a draft request to the preferred supplier or PunchOut item for the owner to submit. Businesses that run warehouse inventory use [Pier](https://github.com/just-every/run-pier), which can send its own replenishment needs to Purse as requests. **Acceptance evidence:** Record a count below a reorder point; a draft request appears with the preferred item and quantity, and nothing is submitted until a person does.

## Coding, allocations and custom fields

- **CODING-001 — Split allocations across codings.** Any request line, PO line, receipt-matched bill line or non-PO bill line can be allocated across several codings, as Procurify's "Allocate to Account Code(s)" does. Each allocation is a full coding: GL account, department, location, class, project, entity (ACCESS-003) and the line's CODING-002 field values. A line is split by percentage, by amount, by quantity or equally, and the allocations must sum exactly to the line's amount (and, for a quantity split, its quantity); a split that does not is refused with the difference shown and nothing is written or reserved. Amounts are integer minor units under PUR-016: each allocation rounds half-up, and the remainder cent goes to the first allocation, so the allocations always total the line exactly. A request line's split carries to its PO line and from the PO line to its receipt-matched bill line; AP can re-split a bill line, or split one whose PO line had none, with the before and after kept in the audit (AUDIT-001). Admins save named split templates (for example "Copier lease: Denver 50 / Chicago 30 / Austin 20") and can default a supplier, catalogue item or purchase type to one. Each allocation is its own coding everywhere downstream: BUDGET-001 reserves, commits and records actual against each allocation's budget; APPROVAL-001 rules that depend on a coded dimension (department, location, project, GL account, entity or a CODING-002 field) are evaluated for each allocation, so each allocated department's approver is asked where the rule calls for one, and an amount threshold applies to the allocated amount or the whole line as the rule is configured; each allocation posts as its own bill or PO line (or the ledger's distribution line) with its coding on every connection (SYNC-001 to SYNC-008), the bill staying one bill; REPORT-001, REPORT-002, REPORT-004 and AP-003 report each allocation under its own coding. A split changed by an approver edit (APPROVAL-004) or a PUR-009 amendment moves each affected allocation's reservation atomically under PUR-006's concurrency guarantee. A split across entities is refused unless intercompany posting between them is configured. Procurify split items arrive with their allocations (IMPORT-001). **Acceptance evidence:** Split a $3,000 non-PO utility bill 50/30/20 by percentage across the Denver, Chicago and Austin location budgets and approve it: the three budgets' actual figures move by exactly $1,500, $900 and $600, and REPORT-001 shows each amount under its location. Sync the bill to a QuickBooks Online sandbox company and a Sage Intacct sandbox company: each ledger holds one bill with three lines coded to the three locations, and the SYNC-005 tie-out shows zero differences. Split a $1,000 PO line equally across Engineering, Sales and Marketing under a chain whose department approver rule applies: the allocations are $333.34, $333.33 and $333.33, totalling exactly $1,000, each department's approver is asked, and each department's budget reserves its share. Submit a 50/30/15 percentage split, and an amount split totalling $990 on the same $1,000 line: both are refused with the difference shown, through the web and the API, and no budget moves.

- **CODING-002 — Custom header and line fields.** Admins define custom fields, as Procurify's custom fields do: header fields hold one value per request and PO (such as "Event"), and line fields hold one value per request line, catalogue item, PO line and bill line (such as "Grant / Fund"). A field is text, number, date, yes/no, or a list whose values come from a Purse-maintained list or from a dimension pulled from the connected ledger and refreshed under SYNC-005 (a NetSuite custom segment or custom list, a Sage Intacct user-defined dimension, a Business Central dimension, a Xero tracking category, or a QuickBooks Online or Desktop class or location). Per purchase type (INTAKE-001) and entity, a field is required, optional or hidden, with an optional default per catalogue item, supplier or department. The server validates every value: a submission missing a required field, or with a list value that is inactive or not in the list, is refused naming the field. Values travel with the record: a request's header and line values copy onto its PO and PO lines (PUR-008) and onto the receipt-matched bill lines (MATCH-001), they are entered on non-PO bill lines (INVOICE-004), and where a line is split under CODING-001 each allocation keeps its own line values. Changing a value after approval is an approver edit (APPROVAL-004) or a PUR-009 amendment, and admins choose whether each field is material. Custom fields can be APPROVAL-001 conditions and BUDGET-001 budget dimensions, and can filter and group REPORT-001, REPORT-002 (including its CSV, XLSX and OData output) and REPORT-004; API-001 reads and writes them. Each field maps, per connection, to the ledger's own place for it: NetSuite custom segments, transaction body fields (header) or transaction column fields (line); Sage Intacct user-defined dimensions or custom fields; Business Central dimensions; Xero tracking categories (at most two, as Xero allows); QuickBooks Online class or location; and QuickBooks Desktop class or custom field. A value with no mapping in a connected ledger, or one inactive there, holds the record in SYNC-005's error queue with the field and value named, and the record never posts without it. Procurify custom field definitions and values arrive with their types, list values and per-record values, including on adopted open POs (IMPORT-001, IMPORT-003). **Acceptance evidence:** Pull a "Grant" user-defined dimension from a Sage Intacct sandbox company, define a required "Grant" line field listed from it and a required "Event" header field, then raise, approve, issue, receive and bill a request with both: the PO and bill lines carry the values and the Intacct sandbox bill line carries the Grant dimension value. Map the same field to a custom segment in a NetSuite sandbox and sync a second bill: the vendor bill line carries the segment value. Submit a request without a Grant value: it is refused naming the field, through the web and the API. Group REPORT-001 spend by Grant and see the bill's amount under its grant, drilling to the bill; add an APPROVAL-001 condition sending one grant to the grants manager and see it route there. Deactivate a grant value in Intacct and push a bill using it: the bill lands in the SYNC-005 error queue naming the field and value. Import a Procurify fixture with custom field definitions and values on an open PO: the adopted PO carries the values.

## Approvals and budgets

- **APPROVAL-001 — Multi-step conditional approval chains.** Chains combine sequential and parallel steps chosen by amount, department, location, project, category, GL account, entity, CODING-002 custom field values, supplier risk, new-supplier status and the requester's manager hierarchy (from ACCESS-002 or the member record); rules on a coded dimension are evaluated for each CODING-001 allocation of a split line. Sequential steps open one after another; parallel steps open together, and the chain moves on only when every one of them is decided. Each step is either "all of" (every named approver or reviewer must approve, in any order) or "any one of" a named group or pool, as Procurify's approval pools are: an "any one of" step reaches every member at once on every channel, and the first member to approve, reject or return decides it. When a pool step is decided, Purse withdraws it from the other members' queues, updates their Slack and Teams messages to show who decided and when, and refuses their email links, Slack and Teams actions and native-app actions with that reason (APPROVAL-003); the step records the deciding member. Concurrent decisions on one step are serialised, so exactly one decides it and the others are refused. A pool member who is the requester is skipped and the step still needs another member to decide; if no other active member remains, it escalates as APPROVAL-002 says. Reminders, escalation and out-of-office apply to the pool as a whole (APPROVAL-002). The request shows which rule chose each step and why, and an amendment re-evaluates the chain against the new revision. **Acceptance evidence:** Configure a chain that adds finance above $10,000 and IT for software; submit requests either side of each condition, see the route and reason before and after submission, and see an amendment re-route. Make the finance step "any one of" a three-person finance pool: a $12,000 request reaches all three at once, the second member approves from Slack and the step is decided with that member recorded; the other two members' email approve link and Slack approve button are then refused, showing who decided, and the step has left their queues. Two members approving one pool step at the same moment through the API: one decides and the other is refused. A request raised by a pool member reaches only the other two members. An "all of" parallel step naming IT and security stays open after IT approves and moves on only when security also approves.

- **APPROVAL-002 — Approval reminders, escalation and out-of-office.** Pending approvers are reminded by email, Slack, Teams or push on the business's schedule in their time zone; after configured inaction the step escalates to the approver's manager or a named backup. An approver (or an admin for them) sets an out-of-office window with a start, an end in their time zone and a delegate who is an active approver with the same scope; during the window new steps and steps already pending route to the delegate with the reason "out of office" recorded on the step, and when the window ends steps not yet decided return to the approver. The delegate can never approve their own request; such a step goes to the approver's manager. On an "any one of" pool step (APPROVAL-001), reminders go to every member not away, escalation fires when no member has decided by the configured time, and a member's out-of-office leaves the step with the rest of the pool without adding their delegate; only when every member is away does the step go to their delegates. Requesters see who holds their request and since when, and the requester (or the buyer) can send the current holder a one-off nudge with an optional message, at most once per step every 24 hours; the nudge goes through NOTIFY-001's channels, is recorded on the step and does not reset the escalation clock. **Acceptance evidence:** Send a nudge with a message to a pending approver; it reaches their Slack with the message and is recorded on the step, a second nudge within 24 hours is refused with the time the next one is allowed, and the escalation still fires at its original time. Leave a step pending past its reminder and escalation times; the approver is reminded, the backup receives the step, and the requester's view shows the holder and age. Set an approver's out-of-office window for tomorrow, submit a request inside the window and see the step go to the delegate with the reason shown to the requester; leave it undecided past the window's end and see it return to the approver; submit a request raised by the delegate inside the window and see that step go to the manager. Set one member of a three-person pool out of office and submit a request: the pool step reaches the other two members and no delegate; leave it undecided past its reminder and escalation times and see both members reminded and the step escalate once to the pool's backup.

- **APPROVAL-003 — Approve from any channel.** Approvers approve, reject or return from email links, Slack, Teams, the native app or the web, always against the exact revision and current authority; a message for a superseded revision, or for a pool step another member has already decided (APPROVAL-001), cannot act, and the Slack, Teams and native-app message is updated to say why. **Acceptance evidence:** Approve one request from an email link and one from Slack, amend a third after its email was sent and see that email's approval refused.

- **APPROVAL-004 — Approver edits on a pending request.** An approver holding a pending step can correct a request's header and coding without returning it, as Procurify's header editing allows: GL account, department, location, class, project, entity (ACCESS-003), need date, ship-to, the supplier (to another active, purchasable supplier) and notes to the supplier, on the header or per line. Admins choose per purchase type which fields approvers may edit and which are material (by default GL account, department, project, entity and supplier). Quantities, prices and lines are never approver edits; changing them is a return or a PUR-009 amendment. An edit creates a new exact revision attributed to the approver, with the before-and-after values shown to the requester and every earlier approver, and the chain is re-evaluated against it (APPROVAL-001): if only non-material fields changed and the route is unchanged, earlier approvals carry forward with the difference recorded; a material change re-asks each earlier approver whose rule depended on the changed field, and any step the change adds (such as another department's approver) is inserted. Budget reservations move from the old budget to the new one atomically under PUR-006's concurrency guarantee. The editing approver then decides the new revision; an approver who is also the requester can neither edit nor approve it. **Acceptance evidence:** As the second approver on a pending $4,000 request, change its class and need date: a new revision shows the difference to the requester, the first approval carries forward, and the step stays with the editing approver, who approves it. On another request, change the department to one with a different budget owner: the reservation moves from the old department's budget to the new one once, the new budget owner's step is added and the first approver is asked again. An approver's attempt to change a quantity through the API is refused.

- **COMMENT-001 — Internal comments and @mentions.** Anyone who can see a request, travel request (INTAKE-006), PO, invoice, MATCH-002 exception, supplier or agreement can add an internal comment, with attachments under PUR-004's file limits and @mentions of members chosen from a picker. Comments are a separate internal thread: they never go to the supplier and never appear on PO-001's or SUPPLIER-001's links, on PO documents (PO-009) or in PO-002's supplier thread, and agents treat them as data, never instructions (AGENT-010). Commenting never changes a record's state: a pending approval step stays with the same approver at the same queue position and age, and only PUR-007's return decision sends a request back. A mention notifies the person through NOTIFY-001's channels (email, Slack, Teams, push and the in-app list, with recent comments and mentions on their dashboard); a reply from that Slack or Teams thread or email posts back onto the record as a comment by the replying member, through the sign-in binding of INTAKE-002 and INTAKE-003. A mention never widens access: a member mentioned on a record outside their ACCESS-001 scope gets a notification saying who mentioned them and that they lack access, with no record title, amount or comment text, and the commenter is told that the member cannot see the record. Authors can edit or delete their own comments, with every version kept in the audit log; comments travel in AUDIT-001 exports and PUR-012's archives, and ERASURE-001 anonymises their authors while keeping the business record. **Acceptance evidence:** As an approver, comment on a pending request and @mention the requester: the requester is notified in Slack, replies in the Slack thread, the reply appears on the request, and the step is still pending with the same approver and unchanged age. Comment on an issued PO with an attachment: the supplier's test inbox receives nothing, and the supplier link and PO PDF show no comment. Mention a member outside the request's department scope: they are notified without the request's content, and their API read of the request is refused. Export the audit log and an archive, restore the archive into an empty installation, and see the comments and their edit history.

- **BUDGET-001 — Budgets and live budget position.** Budgets are set per department, location, project, GL account, CODING-002 custom field (such as a grant) or combination, by month, quarter or fiscal year (any US fiscal year start), in USD. Each budget shows amount, reserved by pending requests, committed by issued POs, adopted open POs (IMPORT-003) and funded card purchases (CARD-001), actual from matched and synced bills and from settled card transactions (CARD-002), with a PO-011 deposit counted as actual once when its invoice is approved and never again through the final bill's deposit credit, and available; a line split under CODING-001 reserves, commits and records actual against each allocation's budget by its share; requesters and approvers see the position at the moment they ask or decide. Over-budget requests are blocked or routed to a budget-owner step by configuration. **Acceptance evidence:** Set a quarterly department budget, raise, issue and bill purchases against it and see reserved, committed, actual and available move at each step; approve a card-paid request and replay its settled transaction and see committed move to actual with no bill; an over-budget request routes to the budget owner. Issue a $1,000 PO line split 60/40 across two department budgets and bill it: each budget's committed figure moves by $600 and $400, then to actual by the same amounts, and the over-budget check applies to each department's share.

- **BUDGET-002 — Budget import and ownership.** Budgets import from CSV, a spreadsheet or [Easel](https://github.com/just-every/run-easel) and from the accounting system's budgets where available, with named budget owners who receive threshold alerts at configured percentages. Ledger budgets are pulled from QuickBooks Online (Budgets), Xero (Budgets), NetSuite (budgets) and Sage Intacct (GL budgets) when connected, mapped to Purse's department, location, project and GL dimensions for review. **Acceptance evidence:** Import a budget file, assign owners, and cross 80% of a budget; the owner is alerted once and the import is previewed before it writes. Import an Easel budget export from a local [Easel](https://github.com/just-every/run-easel) installation and pull a budget from each of a QuickBooks Online sandbox company, a Xero demo company, a NetSuite sandbox and a Sage Intacct sandbox company; each is previewed with its dimension mapping and writes only after review, and a re-pull of an unchanged budget writes nothing.

## Purchase orders and supplier communication

- **PO-001 — POs sent to suppliers and acknowledged.** Issuing a PO emails it to the supplier's order contact as a PDF with PO-009's content from the business's own sending identity (RER mail relay or the buyer's Postmark account), with a no-account link where the supplier accepts, accepts with changes (dates, quantities, prices), or rejects, and gives a promised delivery date. Acknowledgement status and promised dates show on the PO for the buyer and requester; supplier-proposed changes become a PUR-009 amendment for approval, never a silent edit. Bounces and unopened orders are visible. POs adopted from a previous system (IMPORT-003) are never sent unless a person issues an amendment or explicitly re-sends one. **Acceptance evidence:** Issue a PO to a test inbox, acknowledge it with a changed date from the link and see the date on the PO, then propose a price change and see an amendment created for approval; a bounced address shows as undelivered.

- **PO-002 — Supplier messaging on the PO.** Each PO has a message thread with the supplier: buyer messages go by email, supplier replies to that email thread back onto the PO with attachments, and the requester sees the thread. Messages are retained with the PO and travel in archives. **Acceptance evidence:** Send a question from a PO, reply from the supplier inbox with an attachment, and see the reply and file on the PO for the buyer and requester.

- **PO-003 — Overdue acknowledgement and delivery chasing.** Unacknowledged POs and deliveries past their promised or need date are chased automatically by email on a buyer-configured cadence, escalated to the buyer after a configured number of chases, and visible to the requester with each chase and reply. Overdue receiving is already visible (PUR-012); chasing turns it into follow-through. **Acceptance evidence:** Advance the clock past a PO's promised date; the supplier receives chases at the configured cadence, the buyer is alerted after the limit and the requester's view shows each chase.

- **PO-004 — Change orders and cancellations sent.** Approved amendments and cancellations are sent to the supplier as numbered change orders and re-acknowledged through PO-001's link; the PO shows the acknowledged revision. **Acceptance evidence:** Approve a quantity amendment on an issued PO; the supplier receives change order 1, acknowledges it and the PO shows the acknowledged revision.

- **PO-005 — Blanket, recurring and service POs.** Blanket POs set a spending cap and validity period with draw-downs by release or invoice; recurring POs generate scheduled orders (monthly services, subscriptions); service POs receive by amount, by named milestone (each with a value) or by percentage complete, and the received value is what MATCH-001 three-way matches an invoice against. Before each recurring order is generated, the renewal check compares it with the series' linked agreement (CONTRACT-001) and its budget: if the order would fall after the agreement's end date or inside its notice period, exceed the agreement value or the remaining budget, or the supplier is blocked or lapsed (SUPPLIER-004), the series pauses and the PO owner is asked to continue, amend under PUR-009 or end it, with the reason shown; otherwise the order is generated. Remaining balances count as commitments. **Acceptance evidence:** Create a $24,000 annual blanket PO, invoice against it three times and see the balance fall and an over-cap invoice flagged; a recurring PO generates its next order on schedule. Receive 40% complete on a $10,000 service PO: a $6,000 invoice goes to MATCH-002 exceptions as exceeding the received value and a $4,000 invoice matches. Link a monthly recurring PO to an agreement ending in six weeks: the next order is generated, the one after the end date is not, the series pauses and the owner is asked with the agreement end date as the reason.

- **PO-006 — Shipment tracking.** Supplier acknowledgements, advance ship notices and PunchOut confirmations carry carrier and tracking numbers (UPS, FedEx, USPS, DHL); Purse shows tracking status on the PO and tells the receiver when a delivery is out for delivery or delivered. **Acceptance evidence:** Add a tracking number through the acknowledgement link and replay carrier status events; the PO and receiver show delivered and the receiver is prompted.

- **PO-007 — US sales and use tax on purchases.** Requests and POs carry sales tax by line, the business's resale or exemption certificates can be attached and sent with POs, and untaxed purchases of taxable items in a state where the business owes use tax are flagged for use-tax accrual in the ledger export. Tax rates can come from the supplier's invoice or Avalara AvaTax, which Purse asks for the line's ship-to address and tax code, recording the returned rate and jurisdictions on the line and committing the AvaTax transaction only when the bill is approved. **Acceptance evidence:** Issue a PO with an exemption certificate, receive an untaxed invoice for a taxable item and see the use-tax flag carried into the bill sync. With an Avalara AvaTax sandbox account connected, raise a request shipped to a second state: each line's tax equals AvaTax's returned amount with its jurisdictions shown, and the AvaTax transaction is committed only when the matching bill is approved.

- **PO-008 — Automatic PO creation and sending after approval.** Per purchase type, an approved request can become a PO and be sent (or transmitted by PunchOut) automatically, with the buyer able to require manual review for chosen suppliers or amounts. **Acceptance evidence:** Approve a catalogue request under an automatic rule and see the PO created and sent; approve one above the manual-review amount and see it wait for the buyer.

- **PO-009 — PO document content, labels and terms.** Every PO document (PDF, and the same content behind PO-001's link and in cXML OrderRequest where the format allows) carries: the legal entity's name and the logo configured for the bill-to location; bill-to name and address; ship-to name, address and attention line, with a ship-to per line where lines differ; the supplier's name, address and remit-to; PO number, revision and issue date; payment terms, deposit or prepayment terms (PO-011) and FOB or shipping terms; need and promised dates; lines with supplier part number, description, quantity, unit, unit price, line tax and total, with freight, tax and the order total; notes to the supplier; the buyer's contact; and the business's purchase terms and conditions, printed on the document or attached. Admins set the logo, address labels (which name populates bill-to and ship-to), field labels and standard terms per entity (ACCESS-003) and location, with terms per purchase type where they differ; the applicable tax exemption or resale certificate (PO-007) is attached when the bill-to location and supplier state call for it. A change order (PO-004) shows its revision number and what changed. **Acceptance evidence:** Configure two locations with different logos, bill-to and ship-to addresses and terms, and issue a PO from each with a per-line ship-to on one; each downloaded PDF shows its own location's logo, bill-to and ship-to, the supplier and remit-to addresses, payment terms, dates, line tax and freight, notes and the configured terms and conditions, and the exemption certificate is attached where configured. Approve a quantity change: change order 1's PDF shows revision 1 and the changed line.

- **PO-010 — Manual PO close and reopen.** A buyer can close an issued PO, a line or a remaining balance without sending a cancellation to the supplier (a short shipment the supplier has written off, an order fulfilled under another PO), with a required reason; the remaining commitment is released to the budget, open receipt prompts and chases stop, and later invoices against the closed balance go to MATCH-002 exceptions. A closed PO can be reopened by a buyer, which re-reserves its remaining balance subject to available budget and current authority, with both events audited. **Acceptance evidence:** Receive 8 of 10 units and close the PO with a reason; no message reaches the supplier, the budget's committed figure falls by the two units' value and the overdue chase stops, an invoice for 10 units goes to exceptions, and reopening the PO re-reserves the two units once.

- **PO-011 — Deposits and prepayments.** A PO, or the agreement it draws on (CONTRACT-001), can carry one or more deposit or prepayment terms, as Procurify's deposit billing does: an amount or a percentage of the PO total, a due trigger (on supplier acknowledgement under PO-001, on issue, or on a date) and the prepaid account it posts to (a default prepaid expenses or vendor prepayments account per entity, mapped from the ledger). The deposits on a PO can never exceed its total, a deposit added after issue is a PUR-009 amendment, and the terms print on the PO document (PO-009) and show on the supplier link. A deposit invoice (submitted as a deposit by the supplier through INVOICE-005's PO flip, marked as one by AP, or proposed as one by INVOICE-002's reading and confirmed by AP) matches two-way to its deposit term, not to receipts: it is never held for receipt under MATCH-001 or RECEIVE-001, it is approved for payment automatically within the unbilled deposit amount and its tolerance and goes to MATCH-002 above it, and AP-003 sets its due date from the trigger. It posts to the ledger once (SYNC-001 to SYNC-008) as a bill coded to the prepaid account, or as the ledger's own vendor prepayment where it has one (NetSuite vendor prepayments, Business Central prepayment invoices), and is paid through AP-001 or AP-002 like any bill. Later invoices on the PO match three-way against receipts at their full value, and Purse applies the unapplied deposit balance as a credit line coded to the prepaid account (all of it on the final invoice, or pro rata on progress invoices where the term says so), so the net payable is the invoice less the deposit applied and the bill syncs with that credit line. The PO's billed total counts each deposit once, so INVOICE-003's remaining-balance check treats a final invoice carrying its deposit credit as within balance, and a deposit invoice beyond the unbilled deposit is flagged. BUDGET-001, REPORT-001 and REPORT-004 count a deposit as actual when its invoice is approved and never count it again on the final bill. A deposit not yet applied when the PO is closed or cancelled (PO-010) stays on the supplier's balance (RECEIVE-002) for a refund or credit, and open POs adopted under IMPORT-003 keep their unapplied deposits. **Acceptance evidence:** Issue a $20,000 PO with a 50% deposit due on acknowledgement and acknowledge it. A $10,000 deposit invoice arriving before any receipt is not held for receipt, is approved, and syncs to a QuickBooks Online sandbox company as one bill coded to the prepaid account; a retried push still leaves one bill. After full receipt, a $20,000 final invoice matches three-way, carries a −$10,000 deposit credit line and syncs as a net $10,000 bill; the department budget's actual ends at exactly $20,000 and the dashboard shows $20,000 spend on the PO. A second $10,000 deposit invoice on the same PO is flagged and cannot be approved for payment. The same journey against a NetSuite sandbox records the deposit as a vendor prepayment applied to the final vendor bill.

## Receiving

- **RECEIVE-001 — Receiver prompts and invoice holds.** When a delivery is due, marked delivered by tracking or an invoice is waiting on receipt, the responsible receiver is prompted by email, Slack, Teams or push with a one-tap partial or full receipt; invoices on hold for receipt release automatically when the receipt arrives and continue to matching. The responsible receiver is set per purchase type, location or PO (for example facilities for building services, IT for software and equipment, the requester by default), and a receiver can reassign a receipt to a colleague with the receiver capability. **Acceptance evidence:** Receive an invoice before goods; the receiver is prompted, the invoice is held, and recording the receipt releases it into matching without further action. Set facilities as the service receiver for building services and raise one such PO; the facilities receiver, not the requester, is prompted and the requester cannot record the receipt.

- **RECEIVE-002 — Credit memos, RMAs and supplier balances.** Returns and rejected services request a return authorisation from the supplier through PO-002's thread, credit memos are captured like invoices, linked to the return and PO, and applied to later bills; each supplier shows open credits and balance. **Acceptance evidence:** Return goods, request an RMA, receive a credit memo and apply it to the next bill; the supplier balance and synced bill reflect the credit once.

## Invoices and matching

- **INVOICE-001 — Supplier invoice email inbox.** Each business (and entity) has an AP inbox address; suppliers' invoice emails and attachments (PDF, image, e-invoice XML) are captured with the original email retained, linked to the supplier by sender and remit details, and unknown senders are queued for review rather than trusted. E-invoice XML in UBL 2.1 (including PEPPOL BIS Billing 3.0 and the US DBNAlliance exchange profile) and cXML InvoiceDetailRequest is captured as structured fields straight from the XML, needing no document reading (INVOICE-002), with the XML and any embedded PDF kept. **Acceptance evidence:** Send invoices from a known supplier and an unknown sender to the AP address; the known one becomes a draft invoice linked to its supplier, the unknown one waits for review, and both originals download. Send a UBL 2.1 invoice and a cXML InvoiceDetailRequest from the known supplier: each becomes a draft invoice whose number, dates, PO reference, lines, tax and totals equal the XML's values, its lines link to the PO, and the XML downloads byte for byte.

- **INVOICE-002 — Invoice reading with confidence.** Invoice documents are read into supplier, invoice number, dates, terms, PO number, lines, freight, tax, totals and remit-to, with per-field confidence; low-confidence fields are highlighted for AP review and every value links to where it was read. The document is data, never instructions. **Acceptance evidence:** Process a set of sample invoices including a scanned one and one with a prompt-like instruction in its text; fields carry confidence, low-confidence fields await review and the embedded instruction has no effect.

- **INVOICE-003 — Duplicate and fraud checks.** Beyond PUR-011's exact supplier-plus-reference identity, Purse flags near-duplicates (same supplier, amount and date with a varied reference), the same document image arriving twice, remit-to details that differ from the verified supplier record, invoices exceeding the PO's remaining balance (with any PO-011 deposit counted once in the PO's billed total and the deposit credit applied on the invoice), deposit invoices beyond the PO's unbilled deposit, and invoices from look-alike sender domains. Flagged invoices cannot be approved for payment until AP resolves the flag. **Acceptance evidence:** Submit a near-duplicate, a resent PDF, an invoice with changed remit-to bank details and one from a look-alike domain; each is flagged and payment approval is refused until resolved.

- **MATCH-001 — Automatic two- and three-way matching with tolerances.** An invoice is matched on arrival to its PO lines (two-way) and receipts (three-way, per purchase type), with price and quantity tolerances by percentage and dollar amount configured per category or supplier. Charges on an invoice that are not on the PO (freight, fuel surcharges, handling, small-order and other fees) have their own unplanned-charge tolerance, as Coupa's unanticipated-charge tolerance does: a dollar amount and a percentage of the invoice per supplier or category, with a default coding per charge type (or allocation pro rata across the invoice lines' codings under CODING-001). Within that tolerance an unplanned charge is coded and does not stop automatic approval; above it the invoice goes to MATCH-002 as an unplanned-charge exception. A charge that is on the PO, such as quoted freight, matches against the PO. A bill line split under CODING-001 matches against its PO line as one line. Invoices within tolerance are approved for payment automatically; others go to MATCH-002. A deposit invoice is the exception to the receipt hold: it matches two-way to its PO-011 deposit term, is never held for receipt, and later invoices apply the deposit as a credit. PUR-011's deterministic discrepancy calculation is the basis. **Acceptance evidence:** Process invoices exactly matching, within a 2% tolerance, above it and before receipt; the first two are approved automatically, the third goes to exceptions and the fourth is held for receipt. A deposit invoice within a PO's deposit term, arriving before receipt, is approved and not held (PO-011's journey). With a $50 or 5% unplanned-charge tolerance, an otherwise exact invoice carrying a $35 freight line that is not on the PO is approved automatically, with the freight coded to the configured freight GL account, and one carrying a $120 fuel surcharge goes to exceptions as an unplanned charge.

- **MATCH-002 — Exception queue and resolution.** Each exception is assigned to the responsible buyer, requester or receiver with the price, quantity and receipt context; resolutions are accept variance (with approval above a limit), request credit or corrected invoice from the supplier, short-pay, or correct the receipt, each with a reason kept. Exceptions show age and are reminded and escalated like approvals. **Acceptance evidence:** Resolve one exception by accepting a variance with approval and one by requesting a credit through the supplier thread; reasons, approvals and the final matched state are on the invoice.

- **INVOICE-004 — Non-PO invoices and invoice approval.** Invoices without a PO (utilities, professional services) are coded to GL account, department, location, class, project and CODING-002 fields, or split across several codings under CODING-001, and routed through invoice approval thresholds and escalation, with recurring non-PO suppliers suggested a blanket PO. **Acceptance evidence:** Process a non-PO invoice above the threshold; it routes to the configured approver, escalates after inaction and syncs as a bill once approved. Apply the "Copier lease" split template to a second non-PO invoice: its three allocations route to each location's approver and sync as one bill with three coded lines.

- **INVOICE-005 — Supplier invoice submission from the PO (PO flip).** From PO-001's no-account link, a supplier turns an acknowledged PO into an invoice: lines, quantities and prices prefill from the PO's uninvoiced and received balance, the supplier enters the invoice number, date, freight and tax and attaches its own invoice PDF, and the submission arrives as a structured invoice that needs no reading (INVOICE-002) and goes straight to INVOICE-003's checks and MATCH-001. The supplier cannot invoice above the remaining PO balance without the line being flagged, sees the invoice's status (received, approved, scheduled, paid) and cannot change remit-to details from this form. **Acceptance evidence:** Issue and partly receive a PO, open its supplier link, flip it into an invoice for the received quantity with a PDF attached; it matches three-way and is approved automatically, its status shows on the link, and a second flip that exceeds the remaining balance is flagged.

## Payables and payment handoff

- **AP-001 — BILL handoff.** Approved bills (matched or approved non-PO) are sent to BILL with supplier, remit-to, invoice, due date and attachment, and payment status (scheduled, sent, cleared, failed, voided) returns onto the invoice, PO and supplier. A bill is sent once; a failed payment returns to AP with the reason. Purse never holds funds. **Acceptance evidence:** In BILL's sandbox, hand off two approved bills, pay one and fail one; statuses return, the failure reaches AP and re-sending the paid bill is refused.

- **AP-002 — Ledger and ACH payment handoff.** For buyers who pay from their accounting system, approved bills sync as payable bills (SYNC-001 to SYNC-004) and the ledger's payment status flows back; owner-run deployments can instead use an ACH payment provider such as Dwolla, paying only verified bank details (SUPPLIER-002) and recording NACHA return codes on failure. **Acceptance evidence:** Pay a synced bill in the QuickBooks Online sandbox and see Paid on the invoice; send an ACH payment through the provider sandbox, receive an R01 return and see it on the invoice and supplier.

- **AP-003 — Terms, due dates and cash requirements.** Payment terms (Net 15/30/60, 2/10 Net 30, due on receipt) set due and discount dates; AP sees bills due by week with discounts expiring, and finance sees a cash-requirements view of approved bills plus open commitments by expected invoice date. **Acceptance evidence:** Approve bills on different terms; the due-date list orders them correctly, a 2/10 discount shows its deadline, and the cash view totals approved bills and expected commitments by week.

## Card-paid purchases

- **CARD-001 — Card funding for approved purchases.** A purchase type, request or PO can carry the payment method "card". On approval, Purse asks the business's card provider to create a virtual card or set a spend limit for that request: [Coaster](https://github.com/just-every/run-coaster) natively, Ramp (Developer API: virtual cards and limits) and Brex (Team API: cards with spend controls) through their APIs. The card or limit carries the approved amount plus a configured tolerance, the supplier or merchant restriction, a one-time or recurring interval matching the PO, an expiry and the request's reference, and is issued to the requester or buyer. The approved amount is committed against the request's budget at once; a change of amount needs PUR-009 renewed approval and updates the limit; a cancellation or PO-010 close freezes the card or ends the limit. Purse never sees or stores card numbers. **Acceptance evidence:** Approve a $1,200 software request marked "card"; a virtual card or limit for $1,200 restricted to that merchant appears in the Ramp sandbox, the Brex sandbox and a local Coaster installation with the request's reference, the budget's committed figure rises by $1,200, and cancelling the request freezes the card.

- **CARD-002 — Card transaction reconciliation closes the purchase.** Settled transactions on CARD-001's cards and limits return from Coaster, Ramp or Brex (webhooks with a scheduled pull) and reconcile to the request or PO by card or limit ID: amount, merchant (matched to the supplier), date, receipt image and coding. A receipt missing at the provider is requested from the cardholder. The reconciled amount moves the budget from committed to actual (BUDGET-001), counts in REPORT-001 and REPORT-003 as spend under PO, and closes the PO without an invoice: no invoice hold, receipt-for-invoice hold (RECEIVE-001) or overdue chase (PO-003) fires for a card-paid PO. Transactions above the approved amount plus tolerance, from another merchant, or with no matching request become exceptions for AP. Coding and receipts sync to the ledger through the card provider's own accounting sync, and Purse never posts a card transaction as a bill. **Acceptance evidence:** Replay a settled $1,180 transaction with a receipt from the card provider's sandbox for the card of CARD-001's acceptance; it reconciles to the PO, the budget moves $1,180 from committed to actual and releases the $20 balance, the PO closes, no invoice hold or chase fires, the dashboard shows the spend under the department, and no bill is created at the ledger. A $1,500 transaction on the same card becomes an AP exception.

## Accounting sync

- **SYNC-001 — QuickBooks Online.** OAuth connection pulls vendors, chart of accounts, classes, locations, items, customers/projects and terms; Purse pushes new and changed vendors, POs (as QuickBooks purchase orders), approved bills linked to their POs, and credit memos (vendor credits); bill payment status pulls back. Every pushed record stores its QuickBooks ID and a request idempotency key, so retries and re-syncs never create a second bill. **Acceptance evidence:** In a QuickBooks Online sandbox company, connect, import lists, sync a vendor, PO and bill, retry the bill push after a simulated timeout and see exactly one bill; pay it in QuickBooks and see the status return.

- **SYNC-002 — Xero.** The same scope with Xero contacts, accounts, tracking categories, purchase orders, bills (accounts-payable invoices), credit notes and payments, with Xero IDs stored for idempotency. **Acceptance evidence:** In a Xero demo company, connect, sync a contact, PO and bill twice and see one bill, then mark it paid in Xero and see the status in Purse.

- **SYNC-003 — NetSuite.** Token-based authentication to SuiteTalk REST syncs vendors, subsidiaries, departments, classes, locations, items, purchase orders, item receipts, vendor bills, vendor credits and vendor payment status, with external IDs for idempotency and subsidiary mapping per ACCESS-003 entity; custom segments and custom lists are pulled and CODING-002 field values post to their mapped segments and body or column fields. **Acceptance evidence:** In a NetSuite sandbox, sync a PO with a receipt and bill for one subsidiary, retry the bill push and see one vendor bill, then pay it and see the status.

- **SYNC-004 — Pantry.** [Pantry](https://github.com/just-every/run-pantry) connects natively: vendors, POs, approved bills, credits and 1099 boxes post to Pantry's payables with source links back to Purse, and Pantry's payment and reconciliation status returns. Neither product posts a record the other already holds. **Acceptance evidence:** Connect a local Pantry installation, sync a bill, re-sync, and pay it in Pantry; one payable exists and Purse shows it paid with a link to the Pantry record.

- **SYNC-005 — Sync status, errors and tie-out.** Every synced record shows its sync state, external ID and last attempt; failures (closed periods, deleted accounts, inactive vendors) land in an error queue with a plain reason and a retry; scheduled refreshes pull list changes; and a tie-out report compares Purse's approved bills, credits and payments with the ledger for a period. This applies to every connection, SYNC-001 to SYNC-004 and SYNC-006 to SYNC-008, and the tie-out counts records adopted with existing ledger IDs (IMPORT-004) as already posted. On every connection each CODING-001 allocation posts as its own coded bill or PO line and each CODING-002 field value posts to its mapped target; an unmapped or inactive value holds the record in the error queue with the field named. On every connection a PO-011 deposit posts once to the prepaid account and its application on a later bill posts as that bill's credit line, and the tie-out lists prepaid, applied and unapplied deposits per supplier. **Acceptance evidence:** Push a bill into a closed sandbox period; it appears in the error queue with the reason, succeeds after reopening and retry, and the period tie-out shows zero differences.

- **SYNC-006 — Sage Intacct.** A Web Services (or REST API) connection with the business's sender and user credentials pulls vendors, GL accounts, payment terms, items and the department, location, class, project, customer and employee dimensions plus user-defined dimensions, per entity in a multi-entity company (mapped to ACCESS-003 entities). Purse pushes new and changed vendors, purchase orders as Purchasing transactions where the company uses Intacct Purchasing, approved bills (AP bills) with line dimensions (including CODING-002 field values on their mapped user-defined dimensions, and one line per CODING-001 allocation) and the invoice PDF as a supporting document, and vendor credits (AP adjustments); bill payment status (AP payments) pulls back. Every pushed record stores its Intacct record number and a Purse external ID checked before any create, so a retry never creates a second bill. **Acceptance evidence:** In a Sage Intacct sandbox company with two entities, connect, pull vendors and dimensions, sync a vendor and a bill coded to department, location, class and project for one entity with its PDF attached, retry the bill push after a simulated timeout and see exactly one bill with the attachment, post a vendor credit, then pay the bill in Intacct and see Paid in Purse.

- **SYNC-007 — Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central.** An OAuth connection through Microsoft Entra ID to the Business Central API (v2.0) pulls companies, vendors, G/L accounts, items, payment terms, dimensions and dimension values (global and shortcut dimensions). Purse pushes vendors, purchase orders, purchase invoices with dimension set lines and attachments, and purchase credit memos, and pulls vendor payment status through vendor ledger entries. Every pushed record stores its Business Central ID and Purse's external document number checked before create, so a retry never creates a second invoice. **Acceptance evidence:** In a Business Central sandbox environment, connect, pull dimensions, sync a vendor, PO and purchase invoice with two dimensions and a PDF, retry the invoice push and see exactly one purchase invoice, post a credit memo, then pay the invoice and see Paid in Purse.

- **SYNC-008 — QuickBooks Desktop.** QuickBooks Desktop (Pro, Premier and Enterprise, US editions) connects through the QuickBooks Web Connector with a Purse-issued .qwc file and qbXML: Purse pulls vendors, accounts, classes, items, customers:jobs and terms, and pushes vendors (VendorAdd/VendorMod), purchase orders, bills linked to their POs and vendor credits, and pulls bill payments. Because Web Connector runs on the buyer's own machine on a schedule, each request is queued in Purse with its state; a push stores the QuickBooks TxnID and Purse's reference in the bill's memo and reference fields, and a retry queries for that reference before adding, so a lost response never creates a second bill. The sync status shows when Web Connector last ran. **Acceptance evidence:** Against a QuickBooks Desktop sample company with Web Connector, add the .qwc, run a sync that pulls lists and pushes a vendor, PO and bill, drop the response to the bill add and run again to see exactly one bill, pay it in QuickBooks Desktop and see Paid in Purse after the next run.

## Reporting, contracts and notifications

- **REPORT-001 — Spend and committed-budget dashboards.** Dashboards show spend and commitments by department, project, location, supplier, category, GL account and any CODING-002 custom field over any period, with each CODING-001 allocation under its own coding, including card-paid purchases (CARD-002), adopted open POs (IMPORT-003) and deposits counted once as actual (PO-011), with budget versus reserved, committed and actual, filters, and drill-through from any figure to the requests, POs and invoices behind it. Currencies stay separate unless the owner sets reporting rates. **Acceptance evidence:** With a populated workspace, open the dashboard at 1440 and 390 pixels, filter by department and project, and drill from a supplier's committed figure to the exact POs that sum to it.

- **REPORT-002 — Saved, scheduled and exported reports.** Users save report views (with filters and groupings on any coding or CODING-002 custom field, split lines reported per CODING-001 allocation), schedule them by email (weekly, monthly) to members, and export CSV and XLSX. A read-only OData v4 feed authenticated with a scoped API key (API-001) serves Excel (Power Query) and Power BI, and a Google Sheets connection writes a saved view into a sheet the owner chooses through the Google Sheets API on a schedule; both respect the key's or connecting member's ACCESS-001 scope. **Acceptance evidence:** Save a view, schedule it, advance the clock and receive the email with an attachment matching the view; read the same view through the OData feed with a scoped key and see the rows match, see a key scoped to one department receive only that department's rows, and refresh it into a test Google Sheet through the Sheets connection.

- **REPORT-003 — Procurement performance measures.** Purse reports request-to-PO and approval cycle times, spend under PO (PO coverage), off-contract and unapproved spend found in synced bills, on-time delivery, invoice exception rate and savings, separating negotiated savings from realised savings (AGENT-005). Negotiated savings are the difference between a quote's or agreement's first offered price and the awarded price (PUR-005, SOURCE-001, CONTRACT-001); realised savings are only what approved invoices actually billed below the first offered price for the received quantity. **Acceptance evidence:** With a populated workspace, each measure computes from records that drill through, and an invoice without a PO counts as spend outside PO coverage. Award a quote negotiated from $10,000 down to $9,000 and receive an invoice for 60% of the quantity at the awarded price: the savings measure shows $1,000 negotiated and $600 realised, each drilling to the quotes, award and invoice, and an unsigned discount counts as neither.

- **REPORT-004 — Month-end received-not-billed accruals.** For a period-end date the controller chooses, Purse reports received-not-billed value per PO line, as Procurify's Accruals report and Unbilled PO Items export do: the quantity and value received with an effective received date on or before the cut-off (at the PO line's unit price, or the received value for amount, milestone and percentage-complete service receiving under PO-005), less the quantity and value billed on bills approved with an invoice date on or before the cut-off. Bills that are draft, pending approval, in MATCH-002 exceptions or disputed do not reduce it. Receivers record an effective received date with each receipt, defaulting to the day it is recorded and never in the future or before the PO was issued, and PUR-010's recorded time is kept alongside it. Each row carries the PO and line, supplier, item, the receipt's effective received date and recorded date (a receipt recorded after the period end with an effective date inside it is marked late-entered), quantities, unit price, accrual value, currency, and the line's GL account, department, location, class, project, entity and CODING-002 field coding, with one row per CODING-001 allocation of a split line; unapplied PO-011 deposits on the line's PO show in their own column and never net against the accrual. Card-paid POs (CARD-002) and balances closed under PO-010 on or before the cut-off are excluded, and POs adopted under IMPORT-003 count at their imported received and billed amounts and dates. The report filters by entity, department, location, supplier and GL account, totals by GL account and coding, drills from every total to its receipt and bill records, and exports to CSV, XLSX and a Xero manual-journal import file. The controller can optionally post it to the connected ledger (SYNC-001 to SYNC-008, or [Pantry](https://github.com/just-every/run-pantry) under SYNC-004) as one journal entry per entity, debiting each line's expense or asset account by its coding and crediting the configured accrued liabilities account, dated the period end, with a reversal dated the first day of the next period (the ledger's own reversal date where it has one, as NetSuite and Sage Intacct do, and a second dated journal elsewhere). Posting is a person's action under ACCESS-001's finance permission, never automatic; a period posts once under an idempotent reference, and a rerun after later changes shows the difference to post. Where NetSuite's own receipt accruals are in use (item receipts synced under SYNC-003), journal posting is off by default so the accrual is never booked twice. **Acceptance evidence:** On a PO for 10 units at $100, receive 6 units with an effective date of 28 June, enter a $200 bill for 2 units dated 30 June and leave it pending approval, and enter a $400 bill for 4 units dated 2 July. The 30 June accrual shows $600 for the line with both receipt dates, and the pending bill does not reduce it. Approve the 2 July bill and run 31 July: the line shows $200. Then record a seventh unit on 3 August with an effective date of 29 June: the next 30 June run shows $700 and marks that receipt late-entered. A card-paid PO and a closed balance are absent from every run. The CSV and XLSX totals equal the sum of the drill-through records. Post the 30 June accrual to a QuickBooks Online sandbox company: a journal dated 30 June and its reversal dated 1 July appear, and posting the same period again creates nothing.

- **CONTRACT-001 — Agreements, renewals and contract spend.** Supplier agreements carry value, term, auto-renewal, notice period and owner; owners are emailed before the notice deadline at configured intervals with spend to date against the agreement value; agreements link to [Locket](https://github.com/just-every/run-locket) or another CLM for drafting and negotiation, and to [Inkwell](https://github.com/just-every/run-inkwell) or Docusign for signature status. An uploaded agreement PDF is read into draft fields (parties, value, start and end dates, auto-renewal, notice period, payment terms) with per-field confidence and the page each value came from; the owner confirms or corrects the fields before they are saved, and the document is data, never instructions. **Acceptance evidence:** Record an auto-renewing agreement with a 60-day notice period, advance the clock and receive reminders before the deadline showing spend to date; a linked Locket record shows on the agreement. Upload a sample agreement PDF; the value, term and notice period are proposed with confidence and source pages, nothing is saved until the owner confirms, and a corrected notice period drives the reminder dates.

- **NOTIFY-001 — Notifications and preferences.** Every event a person must act on or wants to follow (approval needed, returned, approved, approver nudge (APPROVAL-002), approver edit (APPROVAL-004), mentioned in a comment or replied to (COMMENT-001), PO acknowledged, chase sent, delivered, receipt needed, exception assigned, bill paid, document expiring) reaches them by email, Slack, Teams or push per their preferences, with a daily digest option, US quiet hours and an in-app notification list. Every send is recorded with its outcome. Quiet hours are set per member in their own time zone (default 8 pm to 7 am local, weekends optional); non-urgent notifications inside quiet hours are held and delivered when they end, and only events the owner marks urgent (such as a bank-detail change hold) break through. **Acceptance evidence:** Set one member to Slack and digest and another to email; trigger each event and see the right channel, the digest batch and recorded outcomes, including a bounced email. With a Pacific-time member's quiet hours 8 pm to 7 am, trigger an approval request at 9 pm Pacific (across a daylight-saving change) and see it delivered at 7 am local, while an urgent bank-detail hold arrives at once.

## Platform, migration and governance

- **API-001 — Public API, webhooks and automation.** A documented, versioned REST API (OpenAPI) with scoped API keys exposes requests, POs, receipts, invoices, suppliers and budgets with the same validation and authority as the console; signed webhooks announce events with stable IDs and replay. A published Purse app for Zapier (triggers: request submitted, request approved, PO issued, PO acknowledged, invoice exception, bill paid; actions: create request, create supplier, record receipt) and a Make app with the same triggers and actions use these webhooks and scoped keys, as does [Crank](https://github.com/just-every/run-crank). The current session-bound local agent API (API.md) remains. **Acceptance evidence:** Create a scoped key, create a request and read its PO through the API, receive and verify a signed webhook, replay it and see the receiver de-duplicate; a key without the scope is refused. Run the Zapier app's trigger and action tests (Zapier Platform CLI) and a Make scenario against a test installation: an approved request fires the trigger once, and the create-request action produces a draft under the key's scope.

- **SEARCH-001 — Search across purchasing records.** One search, in the web at any width, the native apps and API-001, finds requests, POs (including IMPORT-003's adopted numbers) and change orders, receipts, invoices and bills, suppliers, agreements and catalogue items. It searches by number (request, PO, change order, Purse invoice, the supplier's invoice reference), supplier name or DBA, amount (exact or a range), date range, requester, status, coding and CODING-002 field values, and by text in descriptions, notes, COMMENT-001 comments and the text read from attached documents (INVOICE-002, SOURCE-002). Partial numbers match, and supplier names match regardless of case and punctuation. Results show only records inside the searcher's ACCESS-001 scope and open the record. SECURITY-001's protected fields are never indexed, so a TIN or bank account number finds nothing. **Acceptance evidence:** In a workspace of 100,000 synthetic records, search for an adopted Procurify PO number, a supplier invoice reference, a supplier name typed without its punctuation, an amount of $1,234.56 and a phrase from an invoice PDF: each returns its record within one second at 1440 and 390 pixels and through the API. A department-scoped requester's search for another department's PO number returns nothing, and a search for a supplier's full bank account number returns nothing for every role.

- **IMPORT-001 — Procurify migration.** Purse moves a Procurify customer in from two named sources, used together or alone: the CSV data sets a Procurify Superuser (or a user with the Export Data permission) downloads from Settings → Export Data (Purchase Order Items, Unbilled PO Items, Receiving Logs, Bills in the detailed custom format, and the user, department, location, account code, vendor, catalogue and budget lists), and the Procurify REST API, connected with the buyer's own OAuth 2.0 client credentials, which reads the same records plus PO headers, approval routing, bill and PO attachments and payment records. Purse imports users, departments, locations, GL accounts, vendors, catalogue items, approval routing (mapped to APPROVAL-001 chains for review, with Procurify approval pools arriving as "any one of" steps with their members), custom header and line field definitions and values (as CODING-002 fields, with list values and ledger mappings), split items' account-code allocations (as CODING-001 allocations), budgets, open and historical POs with receipts, PO deposits and deposit bills (as PO-011 terms with their applied and unapplied balances), bills, vendor credits, payments, request and PO comments (as COMMENT-001 history), travel requests (as INTAKE-006 records, with approved trips not yet expensed keeping their budget reservation) and attachments. Historical POs and bills arrive as inert history; open POs are adopted under IMPORT-003, and records Procurify already synced to the ledger keep their ledger identity under IMPORT-004. Counts and totals reconcile per supplier, department and period against the sources before switching. Procurify expense reports and reimbursements are not purchasing records: Purse lists them in the reconciliation and exports them in the import format of [Coaster](https://github.com/just-every/run-coaster) (or as CSV for Expensify, Ramp or Brex) for the expense tool to take on. PUR-013's review, resume and undo apply. **Acceptance evidence:** Import a fixture built in the documented column layouts of the named Export Data CSVs, and the same records through a recorded Procurify API fixture with attachments; review mappings and counts, commit, reconcile totals per supplier and period against the sources, see expense reports exported in Coaster's format and not imported as purchases, see a travel request arrive as an INTAKE-006 record and a PO with a part-applied deposit arrive with its unapplied balance, see an approval pool arrive as an "any one of" step with its three members, a bill item split across three account codes arrive with its three allocations, and custom field values arrive on their open POs and bills, and undo an untouched batch. An anonymised real Procurify account export run through the same path closes the clause.

- **IMPORT-002 — Coupa, Precoro and spreadsheet migration.** The same reviewed path imports Coupa and Precoro exports and a documented spreadsheet template for businesses moving from email and spreadsheets, and vendor lists pull from the connected ledger (SYNC-001 to SYNC-004 and SYNC-006 to SYNC-008) with SUPPLIER-003's duplicate check. Each source is previewed without writes, committed after review, reconciled by counts and totals per supplier and period against the source, and can be undone like IMPORT-001; open POs are adopted under IMPORT-003 and already-synced records keep their ledger identity under IMPORT-004. **Acceptance evidence:** Import a Precoro export, a Coupa export and the spreadsheet template; each is previewed without writes, committed after review and reconciled to the source's counts and totals, and an untouched batch undoes. Then pull vendors from a sandbox ledger and see duplicates flagged.

- **IMPORT-003 — Adopting open POs without re-sending.** On switch day the owner reviews the open POs from IMPORT-001 or IMPORT-002 and adopts them. An adopted PO keeps its original number (searchable, printed as the PO reference and matched by MATCH-001 and INVOICE-002 against invoices quoting it), its supplier, lines, remaining quantities, received and billed amounts, any unapplied PO-011 deposit, and its remaining value committed against the imported budgets; it becomes receivable and matchable at once and is never sent to the supplier on adoption. The adoption records the source system's approval evidence as imported evidence and the adopting owner and time, never as native authority: any later amendment, change order or cancellation needs native approval under PUR-009 before PO-004 sends it, and new numbering continues without colliding with adopted numbers. **Acceptance evidence:** Import a fixture with an open PO for 10 units, 4 received and 2 billed, and adopt it; no email reaches the supplier's test inbox, the budget's committed figure equals the imported remainder, receiving 3 more units is accepted, an invoice quoting the original Procurify PO number for 3 units matches three-way against the remaining quantity, and an amendment to the adopted PO waits for native approval.

- **IMPORT-004 — Existing ledger records keep their ledger identity.** Vendors, POs, bills, vendor credits and payments the previous system already synced to the ledger are imported with their existing ledger IDs, taken from the source record where it holds them (Procurify's accounting sync IDs) or matched against the connected ledger's vendors and open and paid bills by vendor, reference, date and amount, with every match reviewed before commit. Records carrying a ledger ID are linked, never pushed again: the first sync updates only what Purse changes afterwards, and payment status keeps flowing back for them. Unmatched historical bills stay history and are not posted. **Acceptance evidence:** Import vendors and bills that already exist in a QuickBooks Online and a Sage Intacct sandbox company, review the proposed links, commit and run the first sync to each; zero new vendors and zero new bills are created in either ledger, a payment made afterwards in the ledger returns to the imported bill, and a vendor edited in Purse after the move updates the existing ledger vendor.

- **AUDIT-001 — Audit log and approval evidence.** Every decision (including which pool member decided an APPROVAL-001 "any one of" step), amendment, approver edit (APPROVAL-004), split allocation change (CODING-001), comment and comment edit (COMMENT-001), grant, send, sync, accrual posting (REPORT-004) and payment-status change is in an audit log with actor, time, action and subject, filterable and exportable for auditors (including SOX-style evidence that each PO was approved under the policy in force), surviving erasure of the actor with anonymised references. **Acceptance evidence:** Perform approvals, amendments and sends, filter the log for one PO and export it; erase a member and see their entries anonymised but retained.

- **SECURITY-001 — Protected tax and bank data.** TINs, bank account and routing numbers and connector secrets are encrypted at rest with installation-local keys, masked in the interface except to roles granted them, never included in logs, exports or agent context, and excluded from archives unless the owner explicitly includes them. **Acceptance evidence:** Store a W-9 TIN and bank details, view them as AP and as a requester, export an archive and search logs; only the granted role sees the full values and the archive and logs contain none.

- **ERASURE-001 — Personal data erasure with financial retention.** An owner can erase a departed member's or supplier contact's personal data with a receipt of what was removed, while purchasing and tax records the business must keep (US practice is seven years for payable records) are retained with the person anonymised. **Acceptance evidence:** Erase a former requester and a supplier contact; the receipt lists removed fields, their POs and invoices remain with anonymised references, and a later archive restore does not bring the data back.

## Push further: agents in the purchasing job

Agents do the follow-through that buyers of Zip and Levelpath expect agents to do, and go further by checking whether each purchase delivered what it promised. Every agent works on the business's own records in Purse and connected systems, acts through AGENT-010's governed actions and never approves its own work, commits spend, accepts terms, changes bank details or sends outside the message types a person pre-authorised.

- **AGENT-001 — Requirements intake agent.** In the web form, Slack, Teams or email, an employee describes a need in plain words ("laptops for three new engineers starting 3 November"); the agent asks for what is missing, proposes catalogue, PunchOut or preferred-supplier items, coding, need date and attachments from past purchases and policy, and shows the predicted approval route. The requester reviews and submits; the agent cannot submit or approve. Builds on PUR-014's intake preparation. **Acceptance evidence:** Describe a need in Slack; the agent asks for the missing quantity, proposes catalogue items and coding with sources, and creates a draft only, which the requester edits and submits.

- **AGENT-002 — Quote collection and normalisation agent.** For an RFQ the buyer has approved, the agent sends invitations from the approved template, answers supplier clarification questions from the request's specification (escalating anything else to the buyer), reads replies and quote PDFs, normalises units, freight, tax, lead time, validity and terms into PUR-005's comparison, flags missing or non-conforming items and recommends an award with reasons. The buyer approves the invitation and decides the award. **Acceptance evidence:** Run an RFQ with three test supplier inboxes replying in different formats; the comparison is normalised with sources, a non-conforming quote is flagged, and no award is recorded until the buyer decides.

- **AGENT-003 — Late-order resolution agent.** Within the message types and cadence the buyer pre-authorised, the agent asks suppliers to acknowledge, confirms promised dates, reads replies into the PO, tells the requester, and when a delay threatens the need date proposes alternatives (another supplier, a PunchOut item, a split shipment) with price and date. Substitutions, price changes, cancellations and any binding commitment go to the buyer for approval as PUR-009 amendments. **Acceptance evidence:** Simulate a supplier replying with a three-week delay; the agent updates the PO, informs the requester and proposes an alternative, and nothing changes the order until the buyer approves.

- **AGENT-004 — Invoice exception agent.** For each MATCH-002 exception the agent gathers the evidence (prompts the receiver, reads the PO revision and receipts, asks the supplier for a credit memo or corrected invoice within pre-authorised message types), explains the mismatch in plain words, proposes a resolution, and follows it until the corrected bill posts once in the ledger and payment status settles. Receivers confirm goods, AP approves variances and payments, and bank-detail changes go through SUPPLIER-002. **Acceptance evidence:** Create a price mismatch; the agent requests a corrected invoice, reads it on arrival, re-matches, and the bill syncs once and is marked paid in a sandbox ledger, with every agent step in the audit.

- **AGENT-005 — Value realisation tracking.** A request records its promised outcome (a need met by a date, a seat count used, a quoted saving). The agent collects evidence after delivery (receipts and milestones, software seat usage from the identity provider or [Auth](https://github.com/just-every/run-auth) sign-in records, supplier issues, a requester check-in at 30 and 90 days) and reports realised value against the promise, never counting an unsigned or unrealised discount as savings. Requesters confirm usefulness; the agent cannot invent evidence. **Acceptance evidence:** Approve a 50-seat software purchase promising full use; import seat-usage evidence showing 31 active seats, and see the agent's report state the shortfall with its sources and the requester's confirmation request.

- **AGENT-006 — Recurring-spend consolidation, renegotiation and cancellation proposals.** From supplier families, agreements and notice dates, price history, usage (AGENT-005) and service outcomes (SUPPLIER-005), the agent proposes which recurring spend to consolidate, renegotiate or cancel, each as a reproducible scenario showing the cash effect on commitments and forecast spend, operational risk and the notice deadline. Finance and affected owners approve; the agent then drafts the negotiation brief or cancellation notice for a person to send. **Acceptance evidence:** With overlapping subscriptions and an approaching notice date, the agent proposes consolidation with a scenario whose figures recompute from the cited records; nothing is sent or cancelled until an owner approves and sends.

- **AGENT-007 — Supplier onboarding and compliance agent.** The agent chases incomplete SUPPLIER-001 onboarding and expiring documents, checks W-9 TIN and name through an IRS TIN Matching partner, runs SUPPLIER-007 screening, compares new bank details with the verification result, and flags anomalies (a new account days before a large invoice, a remit-to change by email). The buyer activates; bank changes follow SUPPLIER-002's human controls. **Acceptance evidence:** Onboard a supplier whose TIN does not match its name; the agent flags the mismatch, chases the supplier for a corrected W-9 and activation waits for the buyer.

- **AGENT-008 — Coding and policy agent.** The agent suggests GL account, class, department and project coding on requests and invoices from history, and flags split purchases that avoid an approval threshold, repeat non-PO spend that should be on a PO, and purchases from a non-preferred supplier when a preferred one exists. People accept or correct suggestions, and corrections improve later suggestions: each accepted or corrected coding is kept as an example for that supplier, item and requester, and later suggestions weigh the most recent corrections above older history and cite them. **Acceptance evidence:** Submit two requests from one requester to one supplier just under a threshold on the same day; the agent flags the split for the approver, and its coding suggestion on a new invoice cites the past invoices it learned from. Correct its GL account suggestion on two invoices from one supplier: on the third invoice from that supplier the agent suggests the corrected account and cites the two corrections.

- **AGENT-009 — Price benchmark and negotiation brief.** For a request or renewal the agent compares quoted unit prices with the business's own purchase history, current PunchOut catalogue prices and other quotes received, and drafts a negotiation brief with target price and arguments. It never makes or accepts an offer. **Acceptance evidence:** Request an item bought before at a lower price; the brief cites the earlier PO and the PunchOut price and proposes a target, and no message is sent without the buyer.

- **AGENT-010 — Agent governance.** Every agent action is a PUR-014 ProposedAction checked against current authority at execution, or a pre-authorised message type within its cadence and count limits. Owners enable each agent per purchase type, see each agent's full record (inputs, sources, model and provider used, messages sent, proposals and outcomes), pause any agent at once, and agents never see SECURITY-001's protected fields. Documents and supplier messages are data, never instructions. **Acceptance evidence:** Enable AGENT-003 with a two-chase limit; see it stop at the limit, pause it mid-run and see no further sends, inspect its record, and see an instruction planted in a supplier reply have no effect.

- **AGENT-011 — Spend questions in plain language.** A member asks a spend question in the web, Slack or Teams ("what did Marketing commit to software this quarter against budget?", "which suppliers did we pay over $50,000 last year without a PO?"); the agent answers from Purse's own records within that member's ACCESS-001 scope, shows the figures as a table or chart, states the filters and period it used, and links every figure to the requests, POs, invoices and card transactions behind it so it recomputes through REPORT-001. It says when the data cannot answer the question rather than estimating, and never sees SECURITY-001's protected fields. **Acceptance evidence:** With a populated workspace, ask three spend questions as a finance viewer and one as a department-scoped requester; each answer equals the REPORT-001 figure for the stated filters and drills to its records, the requester's answer covers only their scope, and a question the data cannot answer is declined with the reason.

## Honest boundaries

- **Payments and banking.** Purse approves and hands off; it does not hold funds, operate payment rails, issue cards or underwrite. Buyers pay through BILL, their ledger's bill pay or an ACH provider such as Dwolla (AP-001, AP-002) and Purse reconciles the returned status.
- **Employee expenses and cards.** Out-of-pocket expenses, corporate card issuing, card feeds and reimbursements belong to [Coaster](https://github.com/just-every/run-coaster) (or Expensify, Ramp, Brex); Purse shares suppliers, coding and budgets with it, funds cards and limits for approved card-paid purchases at those providers (CARD-001) and reconciles their transactions back to the request, PO and budget (CARD-002). Travel booking belongs to the business's travel tool (Navan, TravelPerk, SAP Concur Travel), which Procurify does not provide either; Purse holds travel pre-approval and its budget reservation (INTAKE-006), and trip expenses return from Coaster or the expense tool.
- **General ledger.** Purse is not the ledger. Accounts, postings, close and financial statements stay in [Pantry](https://github.com/just-every/run-pantry), QuickBooks Online, QuickBooks Desktop, Xero, NetSuite, Sage Intacct or Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central with explicit synchronisation (SYNC-001 to SYNC-008).
- **Marketplaces and fulfilment.** Purse does not become Amazon or a distributor; it connects the business's own Amazon Business and supplier PunchOut accounts, and suppliers handle inventory, fulfilment and commercial terms.
- **Contract lifecycle and signatures.** Drafting, redlining and clause playbooks belong to [Locket](https://github.com/just-every/run-locket) or another CLM; signing to [Inkwell](https://github.com/just-every/run-inkwell) or Docusign. Purse holds the agreement record, spend and renewal dates.
- **Warehouse inventory and planning.** Stock across warehouses belongs to [Pier](https://github.com/just-every/run-pier); budgets are planned in [Easel](https://github.com/just-every/run-easel) or a spreadsheet and imported. Purse covers stockroom consumables only (STOCK-001).
- **Tax filing and determination.** Purse does not file 1099s or sales and use tax returns; it prepares the data for the ledger or a filing partner (Track1099, TaxBandits, Avalara). It is not tax or legal advice.
- **Risk guarantees.** Screening, TIN checks and bank verification reduce risk; they do not certify a supplier. Legal and risk owners decide, using OneTrust or their own process where they have one.
- **Autonomous commitments.** Agents prepare, collect, chase and propose; they never negotiate binding terms, accept offers, commit spend or change bank details on their own.
- **US first.** Currencies other than USD stay separate and are supported for foreign suppliers, but VAT, GST, e-invoicing mandates outside the US and non-US tax forms are not in this edition.
- **Owner inputs still required.** An anonymised real purchasing case, an actual Procurify account's Export Data CSVs or API access (IMPORT-001) and a current-process cost and attention baseline come from the owner; acceptance that depends on them stays open until supplied. Existing installations keep applied migrations and use the forward upgrade in `UPGRADING.md`.

## Backup and upgrade

A pre-upgrade export restores only with the Purse release that made it. Restore it
into an empty installation of that release, upgrade that installation’s source
in place, run `rer install` and `rer setup`, then export again. Keep the original release
and private export until recovery is verified. Never import an older release’s
archive directly into the current release.
