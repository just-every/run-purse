# Purse product contract

Target edition: 17 October 2026. Purse is connected procurement for growing US businesses (50–500 employees) that replaces Procurify, and gives a buyer of Coupa's mid-market purchase-to-pay or Precoro a complete switch, with the intake orchestration and agent depth buyers look for in Zip and Levelpath. Employees ask for what they need on the web, in Slack or Microsoft Teams, on a phone or from an Amazon Business or other PunchOut catalog; suppliers are onboarded with W-9, insurance and verified bank details, and a supplier a requester asks for that is not yet in the register is proposed on the request and passes a vendor approval chain before anyone can order from it; every line is coded, split across departments, locations, projects and GL accounts where a cost is shared, and carries the business's own custom fields such as grant or event codes; budgets and approval chains (including approver pools where the first member to act decides) hold spend before it is committed; purchase orders are actually sent, acknowledged and chased; goods and services are received; supplier invoices arrive by email, are read, de-duplicated and matched two- or three-way, with deposits billed before delivery and applied once against the final bill; approvers, buyers and AP talk on the record in internal comments with @mentions; controllers close each month with a received-not-billed accrual; card-paid purchases are funded at the card provider and reconciled back to their request, PO and budget; and vendors, POs, bills and payment status stay in sync with QuickBooks Online, QuickBooks Desktop, Xero, NetSuite, Sage Intacct, Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central or [Pantry](https://github.com/just-every/run-pantry) without double posting, including on the first sync after a move from Procurify. Money is USD by default (other currencies stay separate), dates and reminders follow US time zones and daylight saving, and fiscal years, 1099 reporting, sales and use tax and ACH follow US norms.

This is a target contract, not a release verdict: it describes the product Purse is being built towards so the distance can be measured. Every named clause states the behavior a buyer gets and the acceptance evidence (a journey or test through a public door) that proves it. The status table at the end marks each clause `done`, `partial` or `missing` against current code and tests; a cited local test proves the bounded local behavior only, never hosted placement, provider receipt or a real owner export. Clause IDs PUR-001 to PUR-017 are kept unchanged. This documentation round changes no homepage, interface or runtime behavior.

Purse is one product among sibling seeds and keeps the market's separation: [Pantry](https://github.com/just-every/run-pantry) is the general ledger, [Coaster](https://github.com/just-every/run-coaster) owns employee expenses, corporate cards and reimbursements, [Locket](https://github.com/just-every/run-locket) owns contract lifecycle management, [Inkwell](https://github.com/just-every/run-inkwell) owns e-signature, [Pier](https://github.com/just-every/run-pier) owns warehouse inventory, [Easel](https://github.com/just-every/run-easel) owns financial planning, [Auth](https://github.com/just-every/run-auth) is a workforce identity provider and [Linen](https://github.com/just-every/run-linen) is the IT service desk. Purse integrates with each of them, and with their incumbents, rather than rebuilding them.

## Purchasing core (existing guarantees)

- **PUR-001 — Business setup and scoped capabilities.** One business retains locations, cost centers, projects, currencies and native-session requester, approver, buyer and receiver capabilities. Setup preserves identity and data. The full role model is ACCESS-001's. **Acceptance evidence:** Sign in as genuine members, configure the business and capabilities, re-run setup and restart; each member keeps exactly the configured capabilities and no data is lost (`tests/purse.test.mjs` P01).

- **PUR-002 — Supplier register with blocking.** Suppliers retain contacts, documents, agreements and preferred/blocked reasons. Blocked suppliers cannot enter native purchasing. Self-service onboarding, tax and bank details are SUPPLIER-001 to SUPPLIER-003's. **Acceptance evidence:** Create, prefer and block suppliers through the API; submission, approval, conversion and issue to a blocked supplier fail with the recorded reason (P02).

- **PUR-003 — Approved catalog prices.** Approved catalog reference prices copy into request revisions; subsequent catalog changes do not change approved terms. **Acceptance evidence:** Request a catalog item, change its price after approval and see the approved revision and PO keep the original price (P03).

- **PUR-004 — Durable exact request revisions.** Durable drafts retain decimal quantities, project/category/need date, real attachment bytes and immutable revision history. Invalid values are refused. **Acceptance evidence:** Create and amend a request with attachments, download the original bytes and read every revision; invalid quantities and dates are refused without writes (P04).

- **PUR-005 — Quotation comparison.** Supplied quotations compare price, freight, tax, delivery, conditions and evidence, with missing fields visible and currencies separate. Supplier-submitted responses are SOURCE-002's. **Acceptance evidence:** Record quotes in two currencies with a missing freight value; the comparison never merges currencies and shows the missing field (P05).

- **PUR-006 — Exact-revision policy and concurrent budgets.** Current amount/category/project policy binds the exact request revision. Self approval fails. Concurrent budget reservations cannot overspend. Multi-step chains are APPROVAL-001's. **Acceptance evidence:** Approve the exact revision under policy, attempt self approval and race two reservations against one budget; only one fits (P06).

- **PUR-007 — Decisions and scoped delegation.** Approve/reject/return decisions retain reason, actor, time, revision and delegation. Scoped delegation expires. Resubmission preserves history. **Acceptance evidence:** Return, resubmit and approve under an expiring delegation; after expiry the delegate is refused and the history keeps every decision (P07).

- **PUR-008 — Approved lines become numbered POs once.** Approved lines allocate once to numbered POs with source links and downloadable documents, including concurrent conversion. **Acceptance evidence:** Convert the same approved lines concurrently; one PO receives them and its document downloads (P08).

- **PUR-009 — Issuance and amendments under renewed authority.** Document generation and authorized issuance are distinct. Revisions and POs retain approved supplier identity and terms across supplier-directory edits. Supplier/quantity/cost amendments retain issued history, invalidate stale authority, reserve changes and require renewed approval before an amendment is issued. Sending to the supplier is PO-001's. **Acceptance evidence:** Issue a PO, edit the supplier directory, amend supplier and cost; the old issuance remains, the amendment needs fresh approval and reserves its change (P09).

- **PUR-010 — Receiving, returns and commitments.** Partial receipts, service milestones, rejection, return and cancellation retain reasons. Events explain outstanding quantities and commitments. Physical returns exceeding current replenishment authority remain recorded with an explicit hold for review, without overspending. Overreceipt, repeat return and wrong roles fail. **Acceptance evidence:** Receive partially, reject, return beyond authority and cancel; outstanding quantities and commitments reconcile and invalid receipts are refused (P10).

- **PUR-011 — Invoice evidence, discrepancies and accounting CSV.** Supplier ID plus invoice reference identifies invoice evidence once across POs. Identical re-entry is idempotent; conflicting evidence is refused for review. Allocations expose price/quantity/receipt discrepancies and export accounting CSV once without posting or payment. Live posting is SYNC-001 to SYNC-004's and payment is AP-001's. **Acceptance evidence:** Enter an invoice twice identically and once conflicting, allocate across POs and download the CSV; discrepancies show and the conflict is refused (P11).

- **PUR-012 — Commitments, overdue and renewals visible; complete restore.** Commitments remain currency-separated, overdue receiving and renewals stay visible, and complete `.rer` archive restore preserves domain links, decisions, files and import history. Corrupt/nonempty restore fails atomically. **Acceptance evidence:** Export a populated installation, restore into an empty one and compare records and files; corrupt or nonempty restores write nothing (`tests/browser-archive.mjs`, overview in `src/ui/overview.tsx`).

- **PUR-013 — Reviewed CSV move-in.** The documented first CSV move-in kit uses Base import-kit D1, current native-session SQL guards, exact source keys/bytes/mapping decisions (except SUPPLIER-003's redacted TIN/bank cells with original digest and column custody), no-write preview, reviewed commit, resume, idempotence and conflict-preserving undo. Imported status never grants authority. Real incumbent exports are IMPORT-001 and IMPORT-002's. **Acceptance evidence:** Preview, change input, commit, resume and undo a fixture CSV; changed input needs a new review and undo preserves edited targets (P13).

- **PUR-014 — Governed human preparation and proposed actions.** Five human preparation workflows (intake, RFQ, renewal, exception, negotiation) retain editable downloadable drafts, missing fields, source references and input versions. ProposedActions recheck current authority at execution. No result is presented as an agent's unless an agent produced it, and nothing is sent or bound without the authority that action requires. The agents that do this work are AGENT-001 to AGENT-011's, governed by AGENT-010. **Acceptance evidence:** Prepare, edit and download each workflow's draft, then execute a proposed action with stale input and with the wrong role; both are refused (P14).

- **PUR-015 — Owner-run source and command path.** MIT source runs through maintained RER install/setup/dev/build/preview/verify/reset/export/import/deploy-dry-run/describe/check, with native TanStack Start/Hono/Cloudflare, shipped fonts, Purse theme and real dev personas. **Acceptance evidence:** Run the RER command path from a fresh clone and the browser journey (`tests/runtime.mjs`, `tests/browser.mjs`, `tests/run-site.mjs`).

- **PUR-016 — Exact money and quantities with an append-only ledger.** Quantities are thousandths; money is integer minor units, each line rounds half-up once. Commitment reservations/releases/amendments append to an auditable ledger. Invalid/nonfinite values fail. **Acceptance evidence:** Enter decimal quantities and currency text, round lines and amend; the commitment ledger is append-only and invalid values leave state unchanged (P16 and the currency-text test).

- **PUR-017 — Current-session authorization on every door.** Every private API/download checks current native session, capability, business and record scope. Forged actor, role, revision and total cannot authorize. Denials preserve domain state. **Acceptance evidence:** Call every command and download signed out, with the wrong role, forged revision, revoked session and another record's scope; each is refused without mutation (P17).

### Approvals and budgets

- **APPROVAL-001 — Multi-step conditional approval chains.** Chains combine sequential and parallel steps chosen by amount, department, location, project, category, GL account, entity, CODING-002 custom field values, supplier risk, new-supplier status (true while the request's supplier is a SUPPLIER-008 pending supplier) and the requester's manager hierarchy (from ACCESS-002 or the member record; a manager change re-routes steps not yet decided to the new manager); rules on a coded dimension are evaluated for each CODING-001 allocation of a split line. Sequential steps open one after another; parallel steps open together, and the chain moves on only when every one of them is decided. Each step is either "all of" (every named approver or reviewer must approve, in any order) or "any one of" a named group or pool, as Procurify's approval pools are: an "any one of" step reaches every member at once on every channel, and the first member to approve, reject or return decides it. When a pool step is decided, Purse withdraws it from the other members' queues, updates their Slack and Teams messages to show who decided and when, and refuses their email links, Slack and Teams actions and native-app actions with that reason (APPROVAL-003); the step records the deciding member. Concurrent decisions on one step are serialized, so exactly one decides it and the others are refused. A pool member who is the requester is skipped and the step still needs another member to decide; if no other active member remains, it escalates as APPROVAL-002 says. Reminders, escalation and out-of-office apply to the pool as a whole (APPROVAL-002). The request shows which rule chose each step and why, and an amendment re-evaluates the chain against the new revision. ### Agent surface (Base-delivered)

#### BASE-AGENT-001 — One MCP door per seed

*Tier:* Agent surface

**Requirement.** Every seed, hosted or self-hosted, serves one remote MCP endpoint at `/mcp` over Streamable HTTP. It speaks the current revision (2026-07-28) and accepts 2025-11-25 clients through the twelve-month deprecation window. It implements `server / discover`, tools, resources and prompts. Lists are deterministic and carry `ttlMs` and `cacheScope: "private"`. Roots, Sampling and Logging are not served.

**Acceptance.**
1. `server / discover` returns the supported versions, capabilities, `serverInfo` and instructions.
2. A 2026-07-28 request works with no handshake, and a 2025-11-25 client completes `initialize` and lists the same tools.
3. An unsupported version returns `UnsupportedProtocolVersion`.
4. Two `tools / list` calls return the same order, with cache fields.
5. A foreign Origin returns 403, and a header and body method mismatch returns `HeaderMismatch`.
6. An oversized body returns 413 with no effect.

#### BASE-AGENT-002 — The seed's jobs are tools, through the UI's own actions

*Tier:* Agent surface

**Requirement.** Each Core job a person does in the product is a tool. Where one is not, the Agent surface says why: it runs on a device, it is a person's interactive screen, it belongs to a connector or the import door, or it is an installation fact. Risk is never the reason: a risky job is a tool with its class (BASE-AGENT-006). Tools are named `<seed>.<area>.<verb>` and declare input and output schemas and truthful annotations. Each calls the same server action as the UI, so validation and authorization are written once, and the seed's embedded agents use the same registry. A business refusal returns `isError` with the seed's stable error code.

**Acceptance.**
1. The Core clause ids in each tool's `_meta`, with the not-a-tool reasons that `server / discover` serves, cover every Core clause in the contract, and no reason is risk; the harness and `rer check` fail on a gap.
2. Each tool's fixture result validates against its `outputSchema`.
3. Read tools declare `readOnlyHint`; tools that can carry destructive declare `destructiveHint`, and tools that can carry external declare `openWorldHint`.
4. A refusal returns the same error code the UI gets for the same action.
5. The UI action and its tool, on the same fixture, give the same record state and the same audit event type.
6. The seed's embedded agents appear in the audit as calls to the same tools.

#### BASE-AGENT-003 — Records are resources

*Tier:* Agent surface

**Requirement.** Each primary record type has a URI template, and tools return `resource_link`s to the records they touch. A read returns exactly the fields the caller's role sees in the UI. File bytes count against any byte budget on the grant. A record the caller cannot open reads as not found, identical to a missing id. Text from customers, imports or files carries an untrusted marker in `_meta`.

**Acceptance.**
1. `resources / templates / list` names every record type in the Agent surface.
2. Reads as two roles return exactly what the UI shows each.
3. An out-of-scope id and a missing id give identical responses.
4. A write's `resource_link` reads back the new state.
5. Bytes over budget return `budget_exhausted` and no partial content.
6. Customer-sourced text carries the untrusted marker.

#### BASE-AGENT-004 — Business identity and short-lived, audience-bound tokens

*Tier:* Agent surface

**Requirement.** The door is an OAuth 2.1 resource server. Its RFC 9728 metadata names the authorization servers this installation trusts. That is the paired Business when Business provisioned it, verified with the owner-claim trust the installation already holds for its import door; otherwise it is the seed's own sign-in. Access tokens are RFC 9068 JWTs with `aud` set to the seed's canonical MCP URI, `sub` the sponsoring person, `act` the agent (RFC 8693) with its specialist, scopes per tool family, and a lifetime of 15 minutes or less (5 by default). The policy scopes rules on the sponsor, agent and specialist the token names. The consent behind the tokens can last longer and is revocable. Tokens travel only in the Authorization header, and there is no passthrough.

**Acceptance.**
1. An unauthenticated call returns 401 with `resource_metadata`, and the metadata names the trusted servers and scopes.
2. A Business token for this seed works, and the same token sent to a sibling seed returns 401.
3. An expired token returns 401, and a token with a lifetime over 15 minutes is refused.
4. A read-only token calling a write tool returns 403 `insufficient_scope` with every needed scope in one challenge.
5. A token in a query string is refused and not logged.
6. Revoking consent in the seed or in Business refuses the next call within 60 seconds.
7. After unpairing, Business tokens are refused and the seed's own sign-in still works.

#### BASE-AGENT-005 — An agent acts as a person in a role, never more

*Tier:* Agent surface

**Requirement.** Every call is authorized as the sponsoring person's current role and access (BASE-ACCESS-004), intersected with the grant's resources, actions and budget. A grant only narrows. No call widens the calling agent's own authority. A call that would widen its own grant or its sponsor's role, or change the company policy or Pause, is refused with `self_escalation`, whatever the policy says; narrowing its own grant is allowed. Other access and security changes are ordinary actions in the access and security classes. An agent never acts after its sponsor is suspended. Anything an agent reads (records, files, emails, imports, other seeds' results) is data and cannot change its scope, its targets or a call's class.

**Acceptance.**
1. For each role fixture, every tool's allow or deny through MCP equals the UI's for the same action.
2. A one-folder, read-only grant holds, and widening through arguments is refused.
3. Downgrading the sponsor changes the next call's result, and suspending the sponsor returns 401.
4. Calls that widen the agent's own grant or its sponsor's role, or change the policy or Pause, return `self_escalation` and change nothing, even under a policy with every class autonomous; dropping a scope from its own grant runs.
5. An injection fixture yields no out-of-grant call and no change of class: record text asking for a wider action, or claiming that a recipient is internal.

#### BASE-AGENT-006 — Every action declares its risk class

*Tier:* Agent surface

**Requirement.** Classes come from the fleet list in RISK-POLICY.md: standard, external, money, commitment, accounts, gated, access, security, destructive, sensitive and live. The classifier also supplies each class's facts: the measure (items, recipients, amount and currency, people or records), the counterparty (internal, known, new or public), the direction for access, the provider and step for gated, and the data kind for sensitive. A classifier never reads a flag, label or claim supplied by the agent or by record text. A seed cannot add a class; it proposes one for the fleet list.

**Acceptance.**
1. Every tool in `tools / list` carries its possible classes in `_meta`, equal to the Agent surface table.
2. For each tool, fixture calls cover every class it can carry, and the computed classes and facts equal the fixture's expected values.
3. An argument or record text asserting a class or a confirmation (`internal: true`, `confirmExternal: true`) leaves the computed classes unchanged.
4. Money calls carry amount and currency, external calls their counterparty and recipient count, access calls their direction, and destructive calls their item count.
5. No tool in `tools / list` declares a class outside the fleet list, and `rer check` fails on one.
6. A refused call's audit row still records its computed classes.

## Honest boundaries

- **Payments and banking.** Purse approves and hands off; it does not hold funds, operate payment rails, issue cards or underwrite. Buyers pay through BILL, their ledger's bill pay or an ACH provider such as Dwolla (AP-001, AP-002) and Purse reconciles the returned status.
- **Employee expenses and cards.** Out-of-pocket expenses, corporate card issuing, card feeds and reimbursements belong to [Coaster](https://github.com/just-every/run-coaster) (or Expensify, Ramp, Brex); Purse shares suppliers, coding and budgets with it, funds cards and limits for approved card-paid purchases at those providers (CARD-001) and reconciles their transactions back to the request, PO and budget (CARD-002). Travel booking belongs to the business's travel tool (Navan, TravelPerk, SAP Concur Travel), which Procurify does not provide either; Purse holds travel pre-approval and its budget reservation (INTAKE-006), and trip expenses return from Coaster or the expense tool.
- **General ledger.** Purse is not the ledger. Accounts, postings, close and financial statements stay in [Pantry](https://github.com/just-every/run-pantry), QuickBooks Online, QuickBooks Desktop, Xero, NetSuite, Sage Intacct or Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central with explicit synchronization (SYNC-001 to SYNC-008).
- **Marketplaces and fulfillment.** Purse does not become Amazon or a distributor; it connects the business's own Amazon Business and supplier PunchOut accounts, and suppliers handle inventory, fulfillment and commercial terms.
- **Contract lifecycle and signatures.** Drafting, redlining and clause playbooks belong to [Locket](https://github.com/just-every/run-locket) or another CLM; signing to [Inkwell](https://github.com/just-every/run-inkwell) or Docusign. Purse holds the agreement record, spend and renewal dates.
- **Warehouse inventory and planning.** Stock across warehouses belongs to [Pier](https://github.com/just-every/run-pier); budgets are planned in [Easel](https://github.com/just-every/run-easel) or a spreadsheet and imported. Purse covers stockroom consumables only (STOCK-001).
- **Tax filing and determination.** Purse does not file 1099s or sales and use tax returns; it prepares the data for the ledger or a filing partner (Track1099, TaxBandits, Avalara). It is not tax or legal advice.
- **Risk guarantees.** Screening, TIN checks and bank verification reduce risk; they do not certify a supplier. Legal and risk owners decide, using OneTrust or their own process where they have one.
- **Autonomous commitments.** Agents prepare, collect, chase and propose; they never negotiate binding terms, accept offers, commit spend or change bank details on their own.
- **US first.** Currencies other than USD stay separate and are supported for foreign suppliers, but VAT, GST, e-invoicing mandates outside the US and non-US tax forms are not in this edition.
- **Owner inputs still required.** An anonymized real purchasing case, an actual Procurify account's Export Data CSVs or API access (IMPORT-001) and a current-process cost and attention baseline come from the owner; acceptance that depends on them stays open until supplied. Existing installations keep applied migrations and use the forward upgrade in `UPGRADING.md`.

## Backup and upgrade

A pre-upgrade export restores only with the Purse release that made it. Restore it
into an empty installation of that release, upgrade that installation’s source
in place, run `rer install` and `rer setup` inside that existing checkout, then export again. Keep the original release
and private export until recovery is verified. Never import an older release’s
archive directly into the current release.
