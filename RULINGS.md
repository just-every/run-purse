### RL-1 — Legacy invoice evidence

Date: 2026-10-22 · Ruled by: ct · Governs: SECURITY-001, SECURITY-004

Legacy invoice evidence(ct, 22 Oct 2026). Invoice-evidence hashes made under the shared matching root (representation legacy-root-v1) are preserved byte for byte and never recomputed into, replaced by or relabelled as new evidence; new evidence uses a new independently random invoice-evidence key (v2), the old root keeps only its matching use and leaves default archives, and no default archive holds a bank-derived value with the key that derived it. A one-time audited migration gives every legacy hash exactly one state, shown wherever invoice evidence is shown: attested-at-migration when recomputed from retained records (no bank input, or a bank input equal to a retained supplier baseline), labelled "Checked at migration on <date> from retained records. Later checks verify this migration record, not the original submission."; unavailable-source when it binds a bank input no retained record reproduces, labelled "Original bank input not retained – legacy evidence not verified."; did-not-verify when every bound input is retained and recomputation fails, labelled "Legacy evidence did not verify." Only the current native owner re-enters the original bank input, one invoice per no-store POST with reason, evidence reference, native reauthentication within ten minutes and current revision; a match sets verified-by-reentry ("Verified by owner re-entry on <date>. Later checks verify this re-entry record."), a mismatch answers did not verify and writes only a digit-free audit event, and an absent old root returns 503 without fallback. Re-entered digits are discarded; invoices, audit, receipts, logs and archives never hold bank digits, suffixes or bank fingerprints. Each attestation is an HMAC under the invoice-evidence key over the exact legacy hash, representation, retained-field digest, state, event, actor and time. No state claims bank or supplier verification or changes INVOICE-003 flags, reviews, comparisons or holds. Restore writes nothing and names the invoice when a v2 hash does not recompute, an attestation does not bind the stored hash, a state is unsupported by its fields, a migrated archive holds an unmigrated legacy hash, or an invoice carries a bank-derived field; a pre-migration archive is migrated on import by the same rules and refused when a retained-input hash does not recompute. Unavailable-source rows are preserved history and are never counted as verified. The migration receipt counts each state and states that archives exported before migration still carry the old root.

### RL-2 — Erasure and fresh-destination restore

Date: 2026-10-23 · Ruled by: ct · Governs: ERASURE-001

 The following are approved: native-owner explicit inventory/review/consent with fresh proof; resumable withdrawal and deletion of opaque originals; anonymous financial chronology without automatic destruction; encrypted, reveal-restricted retained TIN and bank values; honest verification labels; installation-checkpoint or supplied-journal fencing. **Fresh-destination restores.** A fresh destination given no later journal restores only after the native owner acknowledges "erasure history unverified: supply your latest archive as the journal". The report records that acknowledgment, the archive time and that no journal was supplied. **Retention.** Retained protected values follow an owner retention period in whole years (default 7, minimum 1, IRS Pub. 583 warning below 7) counted from the latest supported payable. Expired values are listed and erased only on owner confirmation. **Coverage.** Order-send acknowledgment names and recipients, travel identities, Pantry-held contact fields and scenario consents are in the reviewed inventory. **Legal adequacy.** None is asserted. **Exact native semantics.** The fresh-destination acknowledgment is a native-owner door in Settings of the empty installation with fresh native reauthentication; it and the restore report (acknowledgment, archive time, no journal supplied) live in an installation-local table that never enters an archive, and a restore that supplies a later journal needs none and is labelled journal-supplied. The unverified label stays on a restored installation; only a restore that supplies the journal clears it. Retention counts whole years from the latest invoice date entered against any order of the supplier; Ready to erase lists protected TIN and bank values and W-9 originals, a supplier with no payable has no clock, and an invoice whose bank binding lost its source value says so. Every refused erasure door of a configured business appends one value-free audit row, and agents cannot propose erasure. A link holder's stated acknowledgment name is erased with its recipient, a sole proprietor's name in a synced Pantry bill source is erased with its contact, and a departed member's scenario consent actor and email are erased with the member.

Deactivation ends sessions and transfers open approvals to the manager or a named delegate, so no request stalls on a departed approver. Native OIDC connections use Base’s pinned issuer/endpoints, signed tokens, PKCE, nonce and current native admission setting; deployment credentials stay private. Native owners explicitly enable provider sign-in, and disabling it ends provider sessions. Provider registration replacement remains unavailable; member associations use the owner-confirmed link door below. SCIM authority is an owner-issued, show-once hashed bearer in an installation-only excluded table. Deactivation settles account status/version, all sessions and outstanding identity tokens, mapped grants and exact request-revision approval handoffs in one transaction. Deactivation is never refused for want of a recipient: status, sessions, identity tokens and mapped grants settle in that transaction; each open approval moves to the eligible current manager, then the owner-named delegate, then the native owner; a step whose only candidate is its requester waits, audited, in the owner's Needs reassignment queue and is never decidable by the departed person. Only a concurrent authority or state change refuses the whole change, before writes. A freshly reauthenticated native owner may link an existing member to an issuer and external ID; it activates on that member's next OIDC sign-in matching both; link and unlink are audited and atomic. A request cannot newly submit to a deactivated SCIM approver. Native authority and buyer issuance recheck the recipient and current transfer; domain and audit history restore without SCIM configuration, bearer, native account bindings or sessions. Future ACCESS-003 entity provisioning, APPROVAL-001/002 approval steps/delegation, API-001 transport, SECURITY-001 credential lifecycle and ERASURE-001 identity retention inherit these current authority, scope and source-history obligations. ### RL-5 — Approver edited-field visibility

Date: 2026-10-22 · Ruled by: ct · Governs: APPROVAL-004

Original recorded outcome and its native context:

An approver holding a pending step can correct a request's header and coding without returning it, as Procurify's header editing allows: GL account, department, location, class, project, entity (ACCESS-003), need date, ship-to, the supplier (to another active, purchasable supplier) and notes to the supplier, on the header or per line. Admins choose per purchase type which fields approvers may edit and which are material (by default GL account, department, project, entity and supplier). Quantities, prices and lines are never approver edits; changing them is a return or a PUR-009 amendment, apart from the buyer's PO-012 edits at conversion within their variance limit. After approval, the requester or an approver may still change the need date as a non-material edit under PO-012, with no re-approval. An edit creates a new exact revision attributed to the approver, with the before-and-after values shown to the requester and every earlier approver, and the chain is re-evaluated against it (APPROVAL-001): if only non-material fields changed and the route is unchanged, earlier approvals carry forward with the difference recorded; a material change re-asks each earlier approver whose rule depended on the changed field, and any step the change adds (such as another department's approver) is inserted. Budget reservations move from the old budget to the new one atomically under PUR-006's concurrency guarantee. The editing approver then decides the new revision; an approver who is also the requester can neither edit nor approve it. Its approver edits must record the before/after values, actor, exact revision, carried or renewed approvals and policy context in AUDIT-001. Acceptance must assert this obligation through its public door and AUDIT-001 read/export. Current native ordered policies reserve at submission and support configured header and line coding, active supplier corrections, class/date carry, material department/project renewal, independent allocation-budget owners, current delegation and per-step SCIM handoff, and audited policy controls. Existing single-approver policies retain their timing. ct 22 Oct ruling: only edited-field before/after values reach the requester and every earlier approver across a lost read grant, including a changed department; private prior revisions and whole-policy snapshots stay withheld in state, command responses, value snapshots and audit. Current read grants do not widen. A material change still renews affected decisions. Different suppliers on individual lines remain refused until supplier-group PO conversion exists; ACCESS-003 supplies entity editing. Native approved need-date corrections retain approval and exact frozen document history; an explicit buyer sends the exact change and the supplier acknowledges that revision without gaining other record access. APPROVAL-001 retains parallel/pool/channel/manager-hierarchy obligations.

### RL-6 — Complete budget positions with private aggregates

Date: 2026-10-22 · Ruled by: ct · Governs: BUDGET-001

Original recorded outcome and its native context:

Budgets are set per department, location, project, GL account, CODING-002 custom field (such as a grant) or combination, by month, quarter or fiscal year (any US fiscal year start), in USD. Each budget shows amount, reserved by pending requests, committed by issued POs, adopted open POs (IMPORT-003) and funded card purchases (CARD-001), actual from matched and synced bills and from settled card transactions (CARD-002), with a PO-011 deposit counted as actual once when its invoice is approved and never again through the final bill's deposit credit, and available; a line split under CODING-001 reserves, commits and records actual against each allocation's budget by its share; requesters and approvers see the position at the moment they ask or decide. A buyer's PO-012 edit at conversion moves the reservation to the edited amount and commits that amount atomically when the PO is issued, per allocation for a split line; an edit that takes a budget over follows the over-budget configuration. Over-budget requests are blocked or routed to a budget-owner step by configuration. Before shipping, REPORT-001 must show period budget versus reserved, committed, actual and available for every supported budget/allocation, without deducting the same purchase twice, with exact source drill-through. Acceptance must assert these figures through the public report door and web. Current native dated department/project/location/GL and native-form custom-field policy budgets select inclusive request need dates within an explicit period of at most one fiscal year. The position retains received-but-unbilled commitments, moves recorded invoice evidence to actual once, and includes imported deposit/net-credit and approved travel-expense evidence without a payment or matched/synced-bill claim. Each supported allocation inherits header/line location and GL or uses its explicit overrides, and reserves, commits and records actual against its own dated policy; its own over-budget configuration applies. Native buyer edits settle the changed amount once and independent budget-owner steps are required for each overage. ct 22 Oct ruling: a requester or approver with access to that budget sees complete amount, reserved, committed, actual and available; purchases outside current read grants appear only as summed “Other purchases you cannot see,” without identifiers, details or drill-through. A viewer without a budget grant sees nothing; no partial sum is labelled complete. Request review and REPORT-001 use those same positions with permitted source links. Native form selectors bind form identity and header/line scope, and combined budgets retain recorded historical identities; ambiguous current mappings refuse. Cross-period dispositions, fully matched/synced bills and native deposits, and CARD-001/002 funded/settled provider evidence remain gaps.

### RL-7 — Travel source and release boundary

Date: 2026-10-23 · Ruled by: ct · Governs: INTAKE-006

Original recorded outcome and its native context:

Travel is a request type alongside goods, services and subscriptions, as in Procurify: an employee (or an assistant for them) requests pre-approval for a trip with the traveler, purpose, origin and destinations, departure and return dates, and estimated costs by category (airfare, lodging, ground transport, meals and incidentals defaulting to the GSA per diem rate for the destination, registration fees, other), with optional attachments such as a conference agenda. The request routes through APPROVAL-001 chains (for example the manager, then finance above a threshold, then a travel or risk reviewer for international trips) and reserves the estimate against the budget (BUDGET-001) like any request. Approval gives the traveler an approval reference to book with; booking itself happens in the business's travel tool (Navan, TravelPerk, SAP Concur Travel) or on a card funded under CARD-001. Actual trip spend returns from [Coaster](https://github.com/just-every/run-coaster) (or an Expensify, Navan, Ramp or Brex expense export) by that reference and moves the reservation to actual; the unspent reservation releases when the trip's expense report closes, or a configured number of days after the return date. A change of dates or a higher estimate is an amendment that re-routes under PUR-009. Current native travel door records the traveler, itinerary, categorized estimates and official FY2025–2027 GSA CONUS M&IE provenance, exact city and county matching and explicitly selected standard rates; unknown destinations require an explicit estimate, never an invented default. Native manager and threshold steps reserve at submit and a new travel revision releases the prior reservation before fresh approval. A manually uploaded original from Coaster’s native claim-detail HTTP door retains its exact bytes and approval reference. Active travelers read their own approved destinations, dates, categorized estimate caps, approval reference, revision and status for assistant-created trips. This booking grant exposes no budget, policy, approvals or originals. The creator and assigned approvers may amend the trip; a traveler without that authority cannot edit it. An owner-configured international reviewer adds an independent final step. Timed release is OFF until the current native owner consents to a versioned business rule naming days after return, a timezone and the consequence of late actual spend. It applies to every approved revision with an exact-revision per-trip override. Rule changes pause unreleased trips; explicit renewed consent re-arms inherited policies, while trip overrides require their own renewed consent. Native owner changes and any business timezone change pause releases. With no business timezone, a consent that omits the zone returns 409. Real Cron releases once, and a later expense report records actual without a second reservation release. The current native owner or a member with request-scoped `request.read` and `payable.approve` may confirm a reviewed source and explicitly close the trip. Coaster reports require independent current native approval. Expensify, Navan, Ramp and Brex configurable CSV exports retain their exact original bytes, an explicitly reviewed column mapping and final-report metadata, including approval independent of the traveler. These manual source declarations are labelled as not independently provider-verified. Every row must carry the exact traveler email, currency and approval reference, and totals must reconcile within the estimate. A repeated exact report cannot repeat actual or release, and a conflicting report is refused. Normalized current report reads follow the request’s existing read grant; the retained original is native-owner-only; import does not fetch a URL or perform an outbound action. INTAKE-002/003, API-001 and IMPORT-001 inherit these revision/reference/source/settlement obligations when they add travel doors; Current reviewed ERASURE-001 consent withdraws affected travel originals and scrubs traveler and assistant identity while retaining anonymous financial facts and hashes; archives retain the explicit withdrawn and unverified state without restoring originals or authority. Travel boundary (ct 23 Oct 2026). The adopted owner source and final consent, owner-only originals, request-scoped normalized reads, default-OFF exact-revision/owner/timezone release consent with the late-actual acknowledgment, and the absence of payment authority are approved as the interim floor. **Traveler reads.** The active traveler reads their own approved destinations, dates, categorized estimate caps, reference and status. They never read budget, policy, approvals or originals. **Release consent.** It names its timezone, and the owner must choose one when the business has none. **Release rule.** A business-level release rule (days, timezone, late-actual consequence) consented once by the owner replaces per-trip consent, with a per-trip override. Rule or owner changes pause unreleased trips. **Report confirmation.** AP and finance roles under ACCESS-001 confirm reports. **Report sources.** Expensify, Navan, Ramp and Brex exports join Coaster.

### RL-8 — Initial purchase-order sending floor

Date: 2026-10-23 · Ruled by: ct · Governs: PO-001

Original recorded outcome and its native context:

Issuing a PO emails it to the supplier's order contact as a PDF with PO-009's content from the business's own sending identity (RER mail relay or the buyer's Postmark account), with a no-account link where the supplier accepts, accepts with changes (dates, quantities, prices), or rejects, and gives a promised delivery date. Acknowledgment status and promised dates show on the PO for the buyer and requester; supplier-proposed changes become a PUR-009 amendment for approval, never a silent edit. Bounces and unopened orders are visible. POs adopted from a previous system (IMPORT-003) are never sent unless a person issues an amendment or explicitly re-sends one. A PO is never issued or sent to a supplier that is not active (SUPPLIER-008). Real supplier acknowledgments and changed promised dates feed PO-003 eligibility before this sending door ships; an acknowledgment stops acknowledgment-only chasing, while overdue delivery still follows its current promise. Its PO sends, outcomes and acknowledgment-status changes must enter AUDIT-001 with the exact issued revision and actor. Acceptance must assert this obligation through its public door and AUDIT-001 read/export. Before shipping, its new document/link/send/cXML/entity/tax-provider/deposit door must preserve PO-009’s frozen applicable identity/profile, labelled fields, original terms/certificate, line destinations/taxes and numbered amendment differences, including structured deposit terms where this capability supplies them. Acceptance must assert PO-009 content and attachments through that public door and its downloaded PDF. PO-001 send boundary (ct 23 Oct 2026). Initial sending inherits PO-004's floor: explicit consent to the exact issued revision and named contact; a link of at most seven days that reads only the document and originals; a stated, unverified name; one claimed native mail attempt with an honest outcome and no automatic retry; explicit human re-send under current `po.issue` scope. Issue-and-send may be one explicit buyer action. **Link replies.** The link records accept, reject with a reason and the promised delivery date directly on the PO, labelled supplier-stated and unverified, and feeding PO-003. Proposed quantity, price, term, item or line-date changes are retained immutably as proposals. A buyer with current scope adopts one into a PUR-009 amendment draft that needs fresh approval, or declines it with a reason. Nothing edits the issued PO. **Re-sending adopted POs.** IMPORT-003 adopted POs are sent only by an explicit buyer re-send. **Automatic sending.** PO-008 automatic sending is permitted only under an owner-consented standing rule (purchase type, supplier/amount manual-review thresholds, contact on file). Rule or owner changes pause it. **Sender.** The interim sender is the owner/admin-configured RER native sender. The buyer's Postmark account inherits SECURITY-001 before shipping.
