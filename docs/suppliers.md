Buyers keep the suppliers and the approved catalogue. Both tabs appear for anyone with the buyer capability.

## Known suppliers

The **Suppliers** tab lists every supplier with its status, reason and renewal date. Choose **Open** to edit one, or fill in **Add supplier**:

- **Supplier name** and **Status**: `active`, `preferred` or `blocked`.
- **Reason**: why it has that status.
- **Contact name** and **Contact email**.
- **Contract reference** and **Renewal date**.

Choose **Save supplier**. Upload agreements and certificates under the supplier's **Evidence files**.

A blocked supplier can't be chosen on a new request or quote. Requests and orders keep the supplier's details as they were when approved, so later edits to the supplier don't change them.

Renewal dates appear on the Overview under **Supplier renewals**, soonest first.

## The approved catalogue

The **Catalogue** tab keeps reference prices for things you buy often. Each item has a description, a supplier, a currency, a **Reference price** entered as currency text (for example, `1,250.50` for AUD 1,250.50), a category and whether it is **Approved**.

When someone drafts a request, they can pick an approved item: its description, price and category are copied into the line. Changing the catalogue later doesn't change requests already made or approved.

## Propose a supplier

Requesters can choose **Propose a supplier** from Requests; buyers can use
Suppliers. Both can propose without creating a purchase request. Check existing suppliers first. Available matches show their
name and status; blocked matches cannot be selected, and private proposals are
referred to the native owner without exposing their contacts. Guided purchase
intake offers the same duplicate check and can select an existing active supplier.

A proposal remains pending. Purchase approval can reserve its request amount,
but conversion and issuance wait for supplier activation. Standalone proposals cannot be
activated: onboarding and verified-bank prerequisites remain required. The existing
guided-intake administrator activation remains available, with independent
review and screening; it does not establish full onboarding or bank verification.

A current native owner who did not propose the supplier can reject it with a
reason. For wholly unissued native requests, rejection returns each affected
request and releases its outstanding recorded reservation once. The request
shows the reason and retains its original approval history. Select another
active supplier in a revised draft, then submit for fresh purchase approval.

Restored decisions are historical evidence. They cannot activate a supplier,
restore a session or repeat a reservation release. Erased proposal contacts stay
anonymous and captured reviews cannot restore them.
