# Procurement record and transport details

The exact record formats, arithmetic, source custody and native workflow boundaries retained from the contract are documented here. Company policy decides agent execution for each declared risk class.

## PUR-001 — Business setup and scoped capabilities

One business retains locations, cost centers, projects, currencies and native-session requester, approver, buyer and receiver capabilities. Setup preserves identity and data. The full role model is ACCESS-001's.

## PUR-002 — Supplier register with blocking

Suppliers retain contacts, documents, agreements and preferred/blocked reasons. Blocked suppliers cannot enter native purchasing. Self-service onboarding, tax and bank details are SUPPLIER-001 to SUPPLIER-003's.

## PUR-003 — Approved catalog prices

Approved catalog reference prices copy into request revisions; subsequent catalog changes do not change approved terms.

## PUR-004 — Durable exact request revisions

Durable drafts retain decimal quantities, project/category/need date, real attachment bytes and immutable revision history. Invalid values are refused.

## PUR-005 — Quotation comparison

Supplied quotations compare price, freight, tax, delivery, conditions and evidence, with missing fields visible and currencies separate. Supplier-submitted responses are SOURCE-002's.

## PUR-006 — Exact-revision policy and concurrent budgets

Current amount/category/project policy binds the exact request revision. Self approval fails. Concurrent budget reservations cannot overspend. Multi-step chains are APPROVAL-001's.

## PUR-007 — Decisions and scoped delegation

Approve/reject/return decisions retain reason, actor, time, revision and delegation. Scoped delegation expires. Resubmission preserves history.

## PUR-008 — Approved lines become numbered POs once

Approved lines allocate once to numbered POs with source links and downloadable documents, including concurrent conversion.

## PUR-009 — Issuance and amendments under renewed authority

Document generation and authorized issuance are distinct. Revisions and POs retain approved supplier identity and terms across supplier-directory edits. Supplier/quantity/cost amendments retain issued history, invalidate stale authority, reserve changes and require renewed approval before an amendment is issued. Sending to the supplier is PO-001's.

## PUR-010 — Receiving, returns and commitments

Partial receipts, service milestones, rejection, return and cancellation retain reasons. Events explain outstanding quantities and commitments. Physical returns exceeding current replenishment authority remain recorded with an explicit hold for review, without overspending. Overreceipt, repeat return and wrong roles fail.

## PUR-011 — Invoice evidence, discrepancies and accounting CSV

Supplier ID plus invoice reference identifies invoice evidence once across POs. Identical re-entry is idempotent; conflicting evidence is refused for review. Allocations expose price/quantity/receipt discrepancies and export accounting CSV once without posting or payment. Live posting is SYNC-001 to SYNC-004's and payment is AP-001's.

## PUR-012 — Commitments, overdue and renewals visible; complete restore

Commitments remain currency-separated, overdue receiving and renewals stay visible, and complete `.rer` archive restore preserves domain links, decisions, files and import history. Corrupt/nonempty restore fails atomically.

## PUR-013 — Reviewed CSV move-in

The documented first CSV move-in kit uses Base import-kit D1, current native-session SQL guards, exact source keys/bytes/mapping decisions (except SUPPLIER-003's redacted TIN/bank cells with original digest and column custody), no-write preview, reviewed commit, resume, idempotence and conflict-preserving undo. Imported status never grants authority. Real incumbent exports are IMPORT-001 and IMPORT-002's.

## PUR-014 — Governed human preparation and proposed actions

Five human preparation workflows (intake, RFQ, renewal, exception, negotiation) retain editable downloadable drafts, missing fields, source references and input versions. ProposedActions recheck current authority at execution. No result is presented as an agent's unless an agent produced it, and nothing is sent or bound without the authority that action requires. The agents that do this work are AGENT-001 to AGENT-011's, governed by AGENT-010.

## PUR-015 — Owner-run source and command path

MIT source runs through maintained RER install/setup/dev/build/preview/verify/reset/export/import/deploy-dry-run/describe/check, with native TanStack Start/Hono/Cloudflare, shipped fonts, Purse theme and real dev personas.

## PUR-016 — Exact money and quantities with an append-only ledger

Quantities are thousandths; money is integer minor units, each line rounds half-up once. Commitment reservations/releases/amendments append to an auditable ledger. Invalid/nonfinite values fail.

## PUR-017 — Current-session authorization on every door

Every private API/download checks current native session, capability, business and record scope. Forged actor, role, revision and total cannot authorize. Denials preserve domain state.

## ACCESS-001 — Procurement roles and buyer limits

Beyond PUR-001's capabilities, a business assigns owner, admin, requester, approver, buyer, receiver, AP clerk, finance viewer and read-only auditor roles, scoped by department, location, project and entity. Requesters see only their own purchases and their department's where granted; AP sees invoices and supplier tax and bank fields only through SECURITY-001's masking; auditors read everything and change nothing. Admins can define custom roles from the same permission set. A buyer grant carries PO-012's price variance limit (dollars, percentage or both, and a quantity variance) per buyer or per buyer role and entity, zero allowed. Every grant, revocation and limit change is audited. Before shipping this capability, its AP and finance roles must replace AP-003’s temporary owner/buyer access with scoped term entry, cash-planning approval and cash-view reads. Acceptance must assert this AP-003 integration through the capability’s public door. Its read-only auditor role must read, filter and export AUDIT-001 history inside its granted scope and must never mutate it. Acceptance must assert this obligation through its public door and AUDIT-001 read/export. Before shipping, REPORT-001 must enforce the new role and department/entity scopes on filters, choices, totals, source drill-through and direct HTTP reads. Acceptance must assert permitted records and refusal or absence of out-of-scope records through the public report door and web. The current scoped receiver sees only an aggregate receipt-matching status on its PO; invoice records, references, amounts and files still require invoice-read permission. Current native implementation uses independent permission-and-scope grants; all dimensions and every line project must fit one complete grant, including the custom role restriction. Requester grants preserve own-purchase reads unless an explicit department-read grant adds department purchases; that read never grants editing another requester’s draft. Native owner authority alone assigns or revokes membership and raises buyer limits. Access administrators may set all price and quantity limits to zero on one existing conversion grant only when its entire effective scope is contained by one complete administrative grant; other grants, identity and active status are retained. Role-wide zero limits require unrestricted administrative authority; only the native owner sets other limit values. Administrators define custom roles without native owner, reveal or assignment authority. Shared supplier/catalogue mutation and unassigned email administration require unrestricted corresponding grants; source reads and purchase changes follow the actual linked request/PO scope. Existing unscoped supplier presence masks remain, while scoped supplier reads require a related permitted purchase; a buyer grant never lends its suffix privilege to another grant. Protected reveal and legacy evidence re-entry remain current-native-owner only under SECURITY-001. Entity scope currently names the main business; ACCESS-003 supplies additional entities and inherits these checks. Role defaults and person overrides use dollar and/or percentage price caps (when both are configured, both apply and the lower governs) plus the quantity limit; decreases remain permitted, the whole edit fits a single current grant, and issuance rechecks authority, limit and budget. Frozen approval revisions stay unchanged; a PO retains attributed before/after conversion evidence and restores only with its exact source and audit proof. PO-012 inherits the remaining edit kinds, channels and authority changes; this native price/quantity conversion door does not close it. ACCESS-002 inherits permission/scope rechecks, native session fencing and audited grants for IdP/SCIM changes; every future source, payment, entity and transport door under SOURCE-001, INVOICE-002/003, AP-003, REPORT-001/003, INTAKE-004, API-001 and ERASURE-001 inherits the same actual-record scope.

## ACCESS-002 — Single sign-on and provisioning

Owners connect SAML 2.0 or OIDC sign-in with Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace, JumpCloud or [Auth](https://github.com/just-every/run-auth), and SCIM 2.0 provisioning creates, updates and deactivates members with their department and manager. Deactivation ends sessions and transfers open approvals to the manager or a named delegate, so no request stalls on a departed approver. Native OIDC connections use Base’s pinned issuer/endpoints, signed tokens, PKCE, nonce and current native admission setting; deployment credentials stay private. Native owners explicitly enable provider sign-in, and disabling it ends provider sessions. Provider registration replacement remains unavailable; member associations use the owner-confirmed link door below. SCIM authority is an owner-issued, show-once hashed bearer in an installation-only excluded table. A freshly reauthenticated native owner explicitly consents to issuer-pinned identity provisioning and department-to-permission templates; all configured scope dimensions apply, unmapped departments grant nothing, provider roles never grant native ownership, and existing native emails are never merged. Provisioning identifies users by issuer plus external ID, and an OIDC sign-in to that provisioned account must match both rather than joining by email; department and manager updates retain their history. Deactivation settles account status/version, all sessions and outstanding identity tokens, mapped grants and exact request-revision approval handoffs in one transaction. Deactivation is never refused for want of a recipient: status, sessions, identity tokens and mapped grants settle in that transaction; each open approval moves to the eligible current manager, then the owner-named delegate, then the native owner; a step whose only candidate is its requester waits, audited, in the owner's Needs reassignment queue and is never decidable by the departed person. Only a concurrent authority or state change refuses the whole change, before writes. A freshly reauthenticated native owner may link an existing member to an issuer and external ID; it activates on that member's next OIDC sign-in matching both; link and unlink are audited and atomic. A request cannot newly submit to a deactivated SCIM approver. Native authority and buyer issuance recheck the recipient and current transfer; domain and audit history restore without SCIM configuration, bearer, native account bindings or sessions. Future ACCESS-003 entity provisioning, APPROVAL-001/002 approval steps/delegation, API-001 transport, SECURITY-001 credential lifecycle and ERASURE-001 identity retention inherit these current authority, scope and source-history obligations. Identity and handoff semantics follow ct’s 22 October ruling D. SAML 2.0 and named provider sandbox journeys remain gaps.

## ACCESS-003 — Multiple entities and subsidiaries

One installation holds several US legal entities (or a US parent with foreign subsidiaries), each with its own EIN, ship-to and bill-to addresses, currency, chart of accounts mapping, numbering and accounting connection. Approvers see one cross-entity queue filtered by entity, and a PO cannot be charged to an entity's budget it does not belong to. Before shipping, its new document/link/send/cXML/entity/tax-provider/deposit door must preserve PO-009’s frozen applicable identity/profile, labelled fields, original terms/certificate, line destinations/taxes and numbered amendment differences, including structured deposit terms where this capability supplies them. Acceptance must assert PO-009 content and attachments through that public door and its downloaded PDF.

## INTAKE-001 — Guided intake forms and supplier proposals

Admins build intake forms per purchase type (goods, software and subscriptions, professional services, contractors, equipment) with conditional questions, required attachments (specifications, quotes, security questionnaires), default coding (with a default CODING-001 split template where a cost is shared), the CODING-002 custom header and line fields that purchase type requires, and a requested need date. The employee path is a single short form that hides fields their purchase does not need, shows which policy and approvers will apply before submission and keeps a draft if they leave. A requester can copy any past request or PO they can see into a new draft ("request again"): lines, supplier, coding, CODING-001 splits and CODING-002 field values copy, catalog lines take the current catalog price with any change from the original shown (PUR-003), and attachments copy only if the requester ticks them; approvals, quotes, receipts and invoices never copy, the draft is a new request routed afresh under APPROVAL-001, and a catalog item outside the requester's current access (CATALOG-001), a blocked supplier (PUR-002) or an inactive coding or field value is flagged and left out until replaced. When the supplier a requester needs is not in the register, the form lets them propose one on the request (SUPPLIER-008) rather than leaving Purse, and the draft and submission carry the pending supplier.

## INTAKE-002 — Slack intake, approvals and updates

A Purse Slack app lets employees start a request with a shortcut or message, attach files, and follow its status in a thread; approvers approve, reject or return the exact revision from an interactive message after sign-in binding; requesters receive PO, acknowledgment, delivery and chase updates. Slack actions use the same server-side authority as the web.

## INTAKE-003 — Microsoft Teams intake, approvals and updates

The same journey as INTAKE-002 through a Teams app (Bot Framework with Adaptive Cards), installable by a Teams admin, with approval cards bound to the exact revision.

## INTAKE-004 — Email intake

Each business has a request address; an employee forwards a supplier quote or describes a need by email and receives a link to a prefilled draft with the attachments retained. Mail from unknown senders is held, never turned into a request. The owner records the address and configures the signed Resend receiving webhook and private provider credentials; recording an address does not verify or configure a mailbox. The current supported door verifies the exact webhook bytes and freshness, hydrates the signed provider email ID through Base's real receiving adapter, and privately retains the exact provider JSON and decoded attachments in R2. The provider-reported sender label never claims verified employee authorship. The subject prefills the draft title, original body/HTML remain source evidence, and the assigned requester completes supplier, coding, lines and terms before explicitly creating one ordinary request draft; submission and approval remain separate native actions. Known senders require current native requester capability (the owner can request); unknown, inactive and non-requester senders are held for the owner's reasoned assignment to an active requester, with no implied grant. Until ACCESS-001 supplies admin, the owner performs administration. Only the owner and current assigned requester read the intake and its exact download-only originals, including after completion; only its assigned requester edits/completes it. Current-session and revision checks apply at each action. Source content is data, never instructions. Provider email identity durably de-duplicates ingestion, and draft-link mail claims before sending with honest adapter outcomes; uncertain claims are visible to the owner and never silently repeated. Bounds are three attachments of 10 MB (10,000,000 bytes) each, 30 MB decoded total, 500 kB combined body and 2 MB provider JSON, refused without truncation. Complete archives retain source bytes, source/request lineage, receipt ledger, holds, corrections, assignments and mail outcomes; archived principals grant no native session. ACCESS-001, API-001 and INTAKE-001 inherit current-role source privacy, explicit completion and replay rules before adding roles, keys or form capabilities; ERASURE-001 inherits the new personal source fields and originals.

## INTAKE-005 — Cross-team review orchestration

A purchase type can require reviews by IT security, legal, privacy or finance in parallel or sequence before approval, with each reviewer's questions, files and decision on the request. Reviews can open and track tickets in Jira, ServiceNow or [Linen](https://github.com/just-every/run-linen), pull a supplier's risk result from OneTrust, and hand contract review to [Locket](https://github.com/just-every/run-locket).

## INTAKE-006 — Travel pre-approval requests

Travel is a request type alongside goods, services and subscriptions, as in Procurify: an employee (or an assistant for them) requests pre-approval for a trip with the traveler, purpose, origin and destinations, departure and return dates, and estimated costs by category (airfare, lodging, ground transport, meals and incidentals defaulting to the GSA per diem rate for the destination, registration fees, other), with optional attachments such as a conference agenda. The request routes through APPROVAL-001 chains (for example the manager, then finance above a threshold, then a travel or risk reviewer for international trips) and reserves the estimate against the budget (BUDGET-001) like any request. Approval gives the traveler an approval reference to book with; booking itself happens in the business's travel tool (Navan, TravelPerk, SAP Concur Travel) or on a card funded under CARD-001. Actual trip spend returns from [Coaster](https://github.com/just-every/run-coaster) (or an Expensify, Navan, Ramp or Brex expense export) by that reference and moves the reservation to actual; the unspent reservation releases when the trip's expense report closes, or a configured number of days after the return date. A change of dates or a higher estimate is an amendment that re-routes under PUR-009. Current native travel door records the traveler, itinerary, categorized estimates and official FY2025–2027 GSA CONUS M&IE provenance, exact city and county matching and explicitly selected standard rates; unknown destinations require an explicit estimate, never an invented default. Native manager and threshold steps reserve at submit and a new travel revision releases the prior reservation before fresh approval. A manually uploaded original from Coaster’s native claim-detail HTTP door retains its exact bytes and approval reference. Active travelers read their own approved destinations, dates, categorized estimate caps, approval reference, revision and status for assistant-created trips. This booking grant exposes no budget, policy, approvals or originals. The creator and assigned approvers may amend the trip; a traveler without that authority cannot edit it. An owner-configured international reviewer adds an independent final step. Timed release is OFF until the current native owner consents to a versioned business rule naming days after return, a timezone and the consequence of late actual spend. It applies to every approved revision with an exact-revision per-trip override. Rule changes pause unreleased trips; explicit renewed consent re-arms inherited policies, while trip overrides require their own renewed consent. Native owner changes and any business timezone change pause releases. With no business timezone, a consent that omits the zone returns 409. Real Cron releases once, and a later expense report records actual without a second reservation release. The current native owner or a member with request-scoped `request.read` and `payable.approve` may confirm a reviewed source and explicitly close the trip. Coaster reports require independent current native approval. Expensify, Navan, Ramp and Brex configurable CSV exports retain their exact original bytes, an explicitly reviewed column mapping and final-report metadata, including approval independent of the traveler. These manual source declarations are labelled as not independently provider-verified. Every row must carry the exact traveler email, currency and approval reference, and totals must reconcile within the estimate. A repeated exact report cannot repeat actual or release, and a conflicting report is refused. Normalized current report reads follow the request’s existing read grant; the retained original is native-owner-only; import does not fetch a URL or perform an outbound action. INTAKE-002/003, API-001 and IMPORT-001 inherit these revision/reference/source/settlement obligations when they add travel doors; Current reviewed ERASURE-001 consent withdraws affected travel originals and scrubs traveler and assistant identity while retaining anonymous financial facts and hashes; archives retain the explicit withdrawn and unverified state without restoring originals or authority. Travel boundary (ct 23 Oct 2026). The adopted owner source and final consent, owner-only originals, request-scoped normalized reads, default-OFF exact-revision/owner/timezone release consent with the late-actual acknowledgment, and the absence of payment authority are approved as the interim floor. **Traveler reads.** The active traveler reads their own approved destinations, dates, categorized estimate caps, reference and status. They never read budget, policy, approvals or originals. **Release consent.** It names its timezone, and the owner must choose one when the business has none. **Release rule.** A business-level release rule (days, timezone, late-actual consequence) consented once by the owner replaces per-trip consent, with a per-trip override. Rule or owner changes pause unreleased trips. **Report confirmation.** AP and finance roles under ACCESS-001 confirm reports. **Report sources.** Expensify, Navan, Ramp and Brex exports join Coaster.

## INTAKE-007 — Integration purchase-request tokens

The native owner creates a token scoped only to creating purchase-request drafts and binds it to one active native requester. Linen ASSET-005 and Pier STOCK-001 inherit this create-only boundary by name; the wider API-001 remains separate. Tokens contain 32 random bytes, are displayed once, and only SHA-256 digests are stored. The owner can rotate or revoke a token immediately; old/revoked tokens cannot create or replay requests. Bearers, preparation and proposed agent actions cannot create, rotate or revoke tokens, read workspace/source records, submit, approve, issue, receive or pay. Token use rechecks the bound native identity and current requester capability; inactive or no-longer-requester identities are refused. The owner cannot silently change the binding: create a new token for a different requester. `POST /api/purse/integration/requests` requires a bearer in the Authorization header, an Idempotency-Key and the ordinary complete purchasing fields. It creates one native draft under the bound requester, recording the token label and requester on every draft, preserving source integration identity and applying normal supplier, catalog, coding, amount and line validation. Client identity, approval and totals confer no authority. As with INTAKE-004, a human reviews and explicitly submits before normal policy approval; creating a draft makes no decision, reservation, PO or send. The same token integration and Idempotency-Key with identical UTF-8 JSON bytes return the original creation response, including after rotation or later human edits; a changed body returns 409 without another draft, and concurrent retries create once. Keys contain 1–200 visible ASCII characters; the body limit is 1,600,000 bytes with no truncation. Tokens and replay-response authority never enter portable archives; complete restore preserves purchasing lineage and AUDIT-001 create/rotate/revoke/use/refusal evidence but requires fresh owner-issued tokens. Every refused use is audited with outcome/reason, door and the recognized integration label/requester when available; invalid or rotated secrets are anonymous, never retained. Secrets and digests never appear in ordinary state, token lists, audit read/export, agent context or URLs.

## INTAKE-008 — Read-only PO-data integration tokens

The native owner creates a token for one named integration and explicitly maps the suppliers whose records it may read. It permits read-only purchase-order headers, lines, status and amounts, bills and payment status for those supplier-mapped records only. AP-001/AP-002 payment-status return is not built: the response states payment status unknown, never infers paid from recorded invoices or cash planning. It exposes no attachments, originals, comments, request records, approval records, payment details or credential data. The token contains 32 random bytes, is shown once and stored only as a SHA-256 digest. It expires after seven days by default and at most 30 days, can be revoked immediately, is rate-limited, and every successful or refused read is audited with the recognized token ID and outcome (unknown secrets remain anonymous). Rotation retains the exact supplier mapping and original expiry; changing either requires new issuance. INTAKE-007 remains create-draft-only and cannot read; the PO-data token cannot create requests or perform any mutation. Locket AGENT-001 inherits this read boundary.

Requesting, approval status, approval and receiving work in the browser at phone width with readable records and no horizontal scroll.

## MOBILE-002 — Native iOS and Android apps

Native apps sign in with the business's identity provider, raise a request (including from a catalog or by photographing a supplier quote, which SOURCE-002 reads into draft lines), push approval, acknowledgment and delivery notifications through APNs and FCM, approve the exact revision, photograph a packing slip or scan a barcode to receive (an item's UPC or GTIN matched to the PO line's supplier part number or catalog item), and queue receipts offline until they reconnect with conflict checks.

## SUPPLIER-001 — Self-service supplier onboarding

A buyer invites a supplier by email, or the invite starts from a SUPPLIER-008 new-supplier proposal (sent by the buyer, or automatically where the business turns on auto-invite), to a no-account onboarding link where the supplier enters legal name, DBA, entity type, address, contacts, remit-to, W-9 (TIN, federal tax classification, backup-withholding certification and e-signature), certificate of insurance, diversity certificates and payment terms. The submission is reviewed and activated by the buyer or, where the business configures one, through SUPPLIER-008's vendor approval chain, with SUPPLIER-002, SUPPLIER-003 and SUPPLIER-007 checks still in force; the supplier can return through a fresh link to update details, which then go back to review. The supplier-link door must preserve SUPPLIER-004’s private single-use document refresh, buyer review, reminder cycles and required-document PO holds.

## SUPPLIER-002 — Bank-detail change controls

Supplier bank and remit details are entered only by the supplier through SUPPLIER-001's link or by AP with evidence, then verified by a call-back to a previously known contact and an independent second approver before use. Account ownership is checked through a bank-verification provider (Plaid Auth with Plaid Identity Match, or the payment provider's own account verification): the account holder name returned by the provider is compared with the supplier's verified legal name and DBA, and a mismatch, an unverifiable account or a provider error blocks activation of the new details until AP resolves it with evidence, keeping the provider, result, score and time on the supplier. Any change emails the previous contact, places pending payments to that supplier on hold and is audited; a change requested by email alone is never applied.

## SUPPLIER-003 — Duplicate supplier detection

New and imported suppliers are checked against existing ones by TIN, normalized legal name and DBA, email domain, address, phone and bank account; possible duplicates are shown before creation and can be merged, keeping both histories. Matching is deterministic: legal names and DBA remove punctuation, spacing and accents and ignore case; email domains ignore case; addresses use the same text normalization; US phones ignore formatting and an initial country code 1. TINs and routing/account pairs use keyed HMAC-SHA-256 with a private, portable business matching key. Native matching-only companion inputs are never stored in full; their fingerprints are excluded from ordinary state, documents and duplicate-review responses, and are never called tax validation, bank verification or certification. CSV TIN and bank columns are accepted and converted to matching fingerprints. The stored original redacts those cell values, preserving every other byte, and custody retains an accompanying JSON artifact with the original-file digest, retained-file digest and redacted-column list: the file digests stay in owner-bound import custody and are excluded from ordinary supplier state. This is PUR-013's one named exception to exact original-byte retention. Native creation with possible matches requires a current explicit review and a reason for keeping a separate supplier; import's exact preview and reviewed commit show existing and same-batch matches. A merge requires a current buyer review and reason, and is refused while either supplier is blocked or pending screening. It keeps both supplier rows and their existing histories: the source is a nonpurchasable alias; immutable approved/issued identities and every PO/invoice link remain unchanged, and the surviving register joins both histories. Source drafts and approvals require a normal supplier amendment and renewed authority; issued receiving and invoice evidence continue against their original POs, and an existing issued PO can close/reopen under the surviving supplier's current holds and ordinary PO-010 authority. Invoice-reference duplicate checks span the merged family. Today's native and CSV doors conform; SUPPLIER-001/002, SECURITY-001, IMPORT-001/002, SYNC-001/002/003/004/006/007/008 and API-001 inherit matching, merge/custody and sensitive-storage obligations before adding full sensitive storage, provider pulls or another creation door.

## SUPPLIER-004 — Document expiry, compliance refresh and renewal reminders

Insurance certificates, W-9s, licenses, security reviews and agreements carry expiry dates; Purse emails the supplier and owner before expiry on a buyer-configured schedule, requests refreshed documents through the standalone supplier-link refresh branch, and can block new POs to a supplier whose required document has lapsed. A buyer records the private original, required flag, expiry, reminder day offsets and optional compliance-cycle days. Dates are inclusive calendar dates in America/New_York; a lapse starts on the following day, and a compliance cycle runs from the last substantive buyer review (schedule-only edits do not restart it). Hourly cron processes the most recently due expiry offset or due cycle once per document version; missed earlier offsets are not sent in a burst. The supplier's first current contact and the configured owner receive separate outcome records; provider acceptance is never called mailbox delivery and local suppressed mail is labelled as such. An uncertain or rejected attempt is retained without automatic resending; a buyer can explicitly request a fresh link. Links use 32 random bytes, retain only SHA-256 hashes, expire after seven days, bind one supplier/document/version, and allow one private file upload only, with no existing-file or register access. Compliance bytes are buyer/owner-only from the moment of upload; ordinary supplier attachments keep their existing scope. A supplier upload stays pending until a buyer reviews its evidence and expiry, so upload alone cannot clear a lapse. Metadata, original files and prior reviews remain in history and portable archives; active bearer links never travel in archives. Required-document holds cover the entire surviving supplier family after SUPPLIER-003 merges and refuse native conversion and issuance while existing issued receiving and invoice evidence remain usable; screening and manual blocks remain independent. This is complete for today's document-refresh branch and native new-PO doors. SUPPLIER-001 inherits this refresh branch before full onboarding ships; PO-001, PO-005, PO-008, PUNCHOUT-001, IMPORT-003 and API-001 inherit the same current required-document hold before adding another PO creation/transmission door.

## SUPPLIER-005 — Supplier performance and issues

Each supplier shows on-time delivery against promised dates, quality rejections and returns, invoice accuracy (match exceptions per invoice), acknowledgment speed, spend and open issues, with issue records (late, damaged, wrong item, billing) linked to POs and resolved with reasons.

## SUPPLIER-006 — 1099 readiness

Suppliers carry 1099 eligibility from their W-9, a default 1099-NEC or 1099-MISC box, and backup-withholding status; payments recorded through AP-001 and AP-002 total by calendar year per box and export to the ledger ([Pantry](https://github.com/just-every/run-pantry), QuickBooks Online, QuickBooks Desktop, Xero, NetSuite, Sage Intacct or Business Central, each vendor's 1099 eligibility and box set in that ledger's own 1099 fields through SYNC-001 to SYNC-008) or a filing partner such as Track1099 or TaxBandits. Purse does not file returns.

## SUPPLIER-007 — Restricted-party screening

New suppliers and changes of name or address are screened against the US Treasury OFAC SDN list (through the public list or a screening provider); possible matches block activation for compliance review, and the screening result and source date are kept.

## SUPPLIER-008 — New-supplier requests and vendor approval

A requester or buyer who needs a supplier that is not in the register proposes one on the request line, as Procurify's "OTHER" vendor and "Request new vendor" do, or from the Suppliers page without a request: name, contact email, optional website, phone and TIN, and the reason this supplier is needed. Before the proposal is saved, SUPPLIER-003's duplicate check runs on the name, email domain, website and TIN and offers any existing match, which the requester can pick instead; a matching blocked supplier (PUR-002) is shown as blocked and cannot be proposed again. A proposed supplier is a pending supplier record whose status is one of proposed, invited, onboarding, in vendor approval, active or rejected, and it is never purchasable until active. The request carries the pending supplier, is submitted and routed under APPROVAL-001 with new-supplier status true, can be approved while the supplier is pending, and BUDGET-001 reserves its amount on approval as for any request. The proposal starts SUPPLIER-001's onboarding invite to the contact email, sent by the buyer or automatically where the business turns on auto-invite. Activation then passes through a vendor approval chain that the admin configures separately from purchase chains, using APPROVAL-001's step types (sequential and parallel, "all of" and "any one of", with conditions on entity, category, supplier risk and 1099 status) with APPROVAL-002's reminders, escalation and out-of-office and APPROVAL-003's channels; SUPPLIER-002's bank verification, SUPPLIER-003's duplicate check and SUPPLIER-007's screening must also clear, and the person who proposed or submitted the vendor can never decide its vendor approval step. ## APPROVAL-001 — Multi-step conditional approval chains

Chains combine sequential and parallel steps chosen by amount, department, location, project, category, GL account, entity, CODING-002 custom field values, supplier risk, new-supplier status (true while the request's supplier is a SUPPLIER-008 pending supplier) and the requester's manager hierarchy (from ACCESS-002 or the member record; a manager change re-routes steps not yet decided to the new manager); rules on a coded dimension are evaluated for each CODING-001 allocation of a split line. Reminders, escalation and out-of-office apply to the pool as a whole (APPROVAL-002). ## CARD-001 — Card funding for approved purchases

A purchase type, request or PO can carry the payment method "card". ## CARD-002 — Card transaction reconciliation closes the purchase

Transactions above the approved amount plus tolerance, from another merchant, or with no matching request become exceptions for AP. Coding and receipts sync to the ledger through the card provider's own accounting sync, and Purse never posts a card transaction as a bill. ## REPORT-003 — Procurement performance measures
