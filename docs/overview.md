## The Overview

The **Overview** tab sits beside Requests, everyone's home. Under the business name it shows:

- **Outstanding commitments**, one card per currency: what's approved and ordered but not yet received or cancelled.
- **Awaiting approval**: how many requests are pending.
- **Overdue receiving**: issued orders past their need date with something still outstanding.
- **Supplier renewals**, soonest first.

Before the business is set up, Requests and the Overview show **Set up business**, which opens Settings for the owner.

## Preparation

The **Preparation** tab is for getting the next action ready by hand. Choose a **Workflow**:

| Workflow | For |
| --- | --- |
| intake | A new need: the evidence, supplier, quantity, project and need date |
| rfq | Asking nominated suppliers for offers by a deadline |
| renewal | An agreement coming up for renewal: keep, change or cancel |
| exception | A problem with an order, receipt or invoice |
| negotiation | Your price and term bounds for a known counterparty |

Fill in what you know, link a **Source record** if there is one, and write the **Editable draft**. Choose **Save preparation**. Purse lists what's still missing, keeps each version, and lets you **Download draft**. People write these drafts; Purse sends nothing and binds no one.

## Audit history

The owner's **Audit** tab lists every change: **When**, the **Actor**, the **Action**, the **Record** and the **Reason**. It includes imports, decisions, receipts and custody claims after a restore.
